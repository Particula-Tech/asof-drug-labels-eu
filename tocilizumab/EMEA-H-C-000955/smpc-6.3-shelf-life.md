# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened vial
3 years

Diluted product
After dilution, the prepared solution for infusion is physically and chemically stable in sodium chloride 9 mg/mL (0.9%) solution for injection.
It can be stored for 24 hours at 30 ºC and for up to 2 weeks in a refrigerator at 2 °C - 8 °C.

From a microbiological point of view, the prepared solution for infusion must be used immediately.
If not used immediately, in use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and would normally not be longer than 24 hours at 2 °C - 8 °C, unless dilution has taken place in controlled and validated aseptic conditions.
