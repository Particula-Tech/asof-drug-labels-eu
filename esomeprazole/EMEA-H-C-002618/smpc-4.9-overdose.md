# 4.9 Overdose

There is very limited experience to date with deliberate overdose.
The symptoms described in connection with 280 mg esomeprazole were gastrointestinal symptoms and weakness.
Single doses of 80 mg esomeprazole were uneventful.
No specific antidote is known.
Esomeprazole is extensively plasma protein bound and is therefore not readily dialysable.
Treatment should be symptomatic and general supportive measures should be utilised.
