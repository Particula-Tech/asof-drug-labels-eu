# 4.3 Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients.

[EMEND 80 mg hard capsules]

EMEND must not be administered concurrently with pimozide, terfenadine, astemizole or cisapride.
Inhibition of cytochrome P450 isoenzyme 3A4 (CYP3A4) by aprepitant could result in elevated plasma concentrations of these medicinal products, potentially causing serious or life-threatening reactions (see section 4.5).
