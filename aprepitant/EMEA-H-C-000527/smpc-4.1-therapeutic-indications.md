# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

EMEND is indicated for the prevention of postoperative nausea and vomiting.

EMEND is also available as 80 mg and 125 mg hard capsules for the prevention of chemotherapy induced nausea and vomiting (see separate Summary of Product Characteristics).

[EMEND 80 mg hard capsules]

Prevention of acute and delayed nausea and vomiting associated with highly emetogenic cisplatin-based cancer chemotherapy.

Prevention of nausea and vomiting associated with moderately emetogenic cancer chemotherapy.

EMEND is given as part of combination therapy (see section 4.2).

EMEND is also available as 40 mg hard capsules for the prevention of postoperative nausea and vomiting (see separate Summary of Product Characteristics).
