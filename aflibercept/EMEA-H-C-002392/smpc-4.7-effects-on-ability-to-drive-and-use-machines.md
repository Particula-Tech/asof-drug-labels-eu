# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

Injection with Eylea has a minor influence on the ability to drive and use machines due to possible temporary visual disturbances associated either with the injection or the eye examination.
Patients should not drive or use machines until their visual function has recovered sufficiently.
