# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store in a refrigerator (2°C to 8°C).
Do not freeze.
Keep the pre-filled syringe in its blister and in the outer carton in order to protect from light.

Prior to usage, the unopened blister of Eylea may be kept at room temperature (below 25°C) for up to 24 hours.
After opening the blister, proceed under aseptic conditions.

[Eylea 40 mg/ml solution for injection in a vial.]

Keep the vial, in the outer carton in order to protect from light.

Prior to usage, the unopened vial of Eylea may be kept at room temperature (below 25°C) for up to 24 hours.
After opening the vial, proceed under aseptic conditions.
