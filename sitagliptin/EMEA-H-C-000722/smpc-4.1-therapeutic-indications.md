# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

Januvia is indicated in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus to improve glycaemic control in combination with metformin when diet and exercise, plus metformin do not provide adequate glycaemic control.
For patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus in whom use of a PPARγ agonist (i.e. a thiazolidinedione) is appropriate, Januvia is indicated in combination with the PPARγ agonist when diet and exercise plus the PPARγ agonist alone do not provide adequate glycaemic control
