# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

VIAGRA 25 mg film-coated tablets

PVC/Aluminium blisters in cartons of 2, 4, 8 or 12 film-coated tablets.

VIAGRA 50 mg film-coated tablets

PVC/Aluminium blisters in cartons or secondary heat sealed card packaging of 2, 4, 8, 12 or 24 film-coated tablets.

VIAGRA 100 mg film-coated tablets

PVC/Aluminium blisters in cartons of 2, 4, 8, 12 or 24 film-coated tablets

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[VIAGRA 50 mg orodispersible tablets]

Aluminium blisters in cartons of 2, 4, 8 or 12 orodispersible tablets.

[VIAGRA 50 mg orodispersible films]

Each orodispersible film is individually packed in a heat-sealed polyethylene-lined aluminium pouch.
Supplied in cartons containing 2, 4, 8 or 12 pouches.
