# 4.9 Overdose

Possible symptoms of overdose include: severe increase in blood pressure, tachycardia, tachycardic arrhythmias, increase in left ventricular end-diastolic pressure with consequent pulmonary congestion to pulmonary oedema, angina pectoris-attacks (especially in patients with known coronary artery disease), nonspecific chest pain, palpitations, nausea, vomiting, sensation of coldness in the extremities and cyanosis.
These conditions can be rapidly reversed by dose reduction or discontinuing the infusion, since dopamine hydrochloride has a half-life of less than 2 minutes in the body.

Should these measures fail, an infusion of an alpha-adrenergic blocking agent, should be considered.
