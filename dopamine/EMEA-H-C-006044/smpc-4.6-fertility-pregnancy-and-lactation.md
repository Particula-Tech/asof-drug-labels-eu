# 4.6 Fertility, pregnancy and lactation

Pregnancy

There are limited amount of data from the use of dopamine hydrochloride in pregnant woman.
Animal studies are insufficient with respect to reproductive toxicity (see section 5.3).
Neoatricon is not recommended during pregnancy and in women of childbearing potential not using contraception.

Breast-feeding

It is not known if dopamine hydrochloride is excreted in human milk.
However, because of the short plasma half-life of dopamine hydrochloride at therapeutic doses no effects on the breastfed infants are anticipated.
Therefore, Neoatricon can be used during breast-feeding.
See section 4.4 for information on prolactin.

Fertility

No data available.
