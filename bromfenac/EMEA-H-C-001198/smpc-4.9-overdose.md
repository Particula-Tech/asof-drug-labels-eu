# 4.9 Overdose

No abnormal findings or adverse reactions of clinical concern were noted upon administration of two drops 2mg/ml solution four times a day for the period of up to 28 days.
Accidental administration of more than one drop should not result in increased topical exposure as excessive volume would rinse out of the eye due to limited conjunctival sac capacity.
There is practically no risk of adverse effects due to accidental oral ingestion.
Ingestion of the 5 ml bottle content corresponds to an oral dose of less than 5 mg bromfenac, which is 30 times lower than daily dose of bromfenac oral formulation formerly used.
If Yellox is accidentally ingested, fluids should be taken to dilute the medicinal product.
