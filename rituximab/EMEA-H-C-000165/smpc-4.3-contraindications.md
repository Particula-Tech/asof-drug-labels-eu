# 4.3 Contraindications

Contraindications for use in non-Hodgkin’s Lymphoma

Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients of this product or to murine proteins.

Contraindications for use in Rheumatoid Arthritis

Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients of this product or to murine proteins.

Active, severe infections (see section 4.4).

Severe heart failure (NYHA Class IV) or severe, uncontrolled cardiac disease.
