# 4.3 Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to murine proteins, or to any of the excipients listed in section 6.1.

Active, severe infections (see section 4.4).

Patients in a severely immunocompromised state.

Severe heart failure (New York Heart Association Class IV) or severe, uncontrolled cardiac disease for use in rheumatoid arthritis, granulomatosis with polyangiitis, microscopic polyangiitis and pemphigus vulgaris only (see section 4.4 regarding other cardiovascular diseases).
