# 4.2 Posology and method of administration

The recommended dosage of MABTHERA used as a single agent for adult patients is 375 mg/m2 body surface, administered as an intravenous infusion once weekly for four weeks.
MABTHERA is suitable for outpatient administration.

Premedication consisting of a pain-reliever and an antihistaminic, e.g. paracetamol and diphenhydramine, may be administered before each infusion of MABTHERA.

First infusion: The prepared MABTHERA solution should be administered by intravenous infusion through a dedicated line.
The recommended initial rate for infusion is 50 mg/hr; after the first 30 minutes, it can be escalated in 50 mg/hr increments every 30 minutes, to a maximum of 400 mg/hr.
If hypersensitivity or an infusion-related event develops, the infusion should be temporarily slowed or interrupted (see 4.4 Special warnings and precautions for use).
The infusion can continue at one-half the previous rate upon improvement of patient symptoms.

Subsequent infusions: Subsequent doses of MABTHERA can be infused at an initial rate of 100 mg/hr, and increased by 100 mg/hr increments at 30 minutes intervals, to a maximum of 400 mg/hr.

Retreatment following relapse: Patients who have responded to MABTHERA initially have been treated again with MABTHERA.
Response rate seems to be comparable in these retreated patients.
