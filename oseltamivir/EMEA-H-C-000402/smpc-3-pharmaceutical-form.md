# 3. PHARMACEUTICAL FORM

Capsule, hard
The hard capsule consists of a grey opaque body bearing the imprint “ROCHE” and a light yellow opaque cap bearing the imprint “75mg”.
Imprints are blue.

Powder for oral suspension
The powder is a granulate or clumped granulate with a white to light yellow colour.
