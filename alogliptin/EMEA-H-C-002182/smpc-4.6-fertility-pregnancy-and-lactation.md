# 4.6 Fertility, pregnancy and lactation

Pregnancy

There are no data from the use of alogliptin in pregnant women.
Animal studies do not indicate direct or indirect harmful effects with respect to reproductive toxicity (see section 5.3).
As a precautionary measure, it is preferable to avoid the use of alogliptin during pregnancy.
Breast-feeding

It is unknown whether alogliptin is excreted in human milk.
Animal studies have shown excretion of alogliptin in milk (see section 5.3).
A risk to the suckling child cannot be excluded.

A decision on whether to discontinue breast-feeding or to discontinue alogliptin therapy should be made taking into account the benefit of breast-feeding for the child and the benefit of alogliptin therapy for the woman.

Fertility

The effect of alogliptin on fertility in humans has not been studied.
No adverse effects on fertility were observed in animal studies (see section 5.3).
