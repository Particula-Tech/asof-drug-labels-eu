# 6.3 Shelf life

3 years.

After dilution in intravenous solutions, TRISENOX is chemically and physically stable for 24 hours at 15-30°C and 48 hours at refrigerated (2-8°C) temperatures.
From a microbiological point of view, the product must be used immediately.
If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and would normally not be longer than 24 hours at 2-8°C, unless dilution has taken place in controlled and validated aseptic conditions.
