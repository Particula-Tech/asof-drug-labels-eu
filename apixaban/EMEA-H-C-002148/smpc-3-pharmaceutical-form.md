# 3. PHARMACEUTICAL FORM

Film-coated tablet (tablet)
Yellow, round tablets (diameter of 6 mm) debossed with 893 on one side and 2½ on the other side.

[Eliquis 5 mg film-coated tablets]

Pink, oval tablets (10 mm x 5mm) debossed with 894 on one side and 5 on the other side.

[Eliquis 0.15 mg granules in capsules for opening]

Granules in capsules for opening.
The granules are white to off white in colour.
They are provided in a hard capsule with a clear body and yellow opaque cap, which must be opened prior to administration.

[Eliquis 0.5 mg coated granule in sachet]

0.5 mg coated granules packaged in 0.5, 1.5, and 2 mg sachets.
Pink colour and round shape (diameter 3 mm).
