# 4.8 Undesirable effects

This section provides information based on all adverse reactions from clinical trials, post-marketing studies or spontaneous reports.
Approximately 47 % of patients experienced adverse reactions in the clinical trial programme.
However, only 0.9 % of patients stopped therapy because of an adverse reaction.
The most frequently reported adverse reactions are hot flushes, nausea, and injection site reactions.
Adverse reactions listed below are classified according to frequency and System Organ Class (SOC).
Frequency groupings are defined according to the following convention: Very common (≥1/10), Common (≥1/100 to <1/10), Uncommon (≥1/1,000 to <1/100).
Within each frequency grouping adverse reactions are reported in order of decreasing seriousness.
