# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

PVC/PCTFE/Aluminium blister packs containing 14 or 56 tablets or multipacks containing 168
(3 packs of 56) tablets.

Not all pack sizes or types may be marketed.

[Jakavi 5 mg/ml oral solution]

Jakavi oral solution is available in 70 ml amber glass bottles with a white polypropylene child-resistant screw cap closure.
Packs containing one bottle of 60 ml oral solution, two 1 ml polypropylene oral syringes and one low density polypropylene press-in bottle adapter.
The oral syringes are equipped with plunger O-rings and printed with 0.1 ml graduation marks.
