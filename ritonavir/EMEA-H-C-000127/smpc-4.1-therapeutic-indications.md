# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

Norvir is indicated in combination with antiretroviral nucleoside analogue(s) for the treatment of HIV-1 infected adult patients with advanced or progressive immunodeficiency.

Clinical endpoint data are only available in patients with advanced HIV-disease.
In patients with less advanced HIV-disease only data based on biological markers such as viral load and CD4 cell count are yet available.
In these patients studies on the effect of ritonavir on clinical endpoints are ongoing.

See section 5.1 for the results of the important studies.
