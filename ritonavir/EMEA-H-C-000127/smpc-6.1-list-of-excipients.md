# 6.1 List of excipients

Norvir oral solution contains: ethanol, purified water, polyoxyl 35 castor oil, propylene glycol, anhydrous citric acid, saccharin sodium, peppermint oil, creamy caramel flavour, and dye E110.

[Norvir capsule 100 mg]

Norvir gelatine capsules contain: saturated polyglycolyzed glycerides, ethanol, polyoxyl 35 castor oil, propylene glycol, medium chain triglycerides, polysorbate 80, and anhydrous citric acid.
The banding components are: gelatine and polysorbate 80.
The printing ingredients are: shellac, blue 2 and titanium dioxide.
