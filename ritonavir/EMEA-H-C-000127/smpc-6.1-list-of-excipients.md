# 6.1 List of excipients

Norvir soft capsules contain: ethanol, butylated hydroxytoluene (E321), oleic acid and polyoxyl 35 castor oil.
The capsule shell components are: gelatine, “sorbitol special” (ie sorbitol sorbitolanhydrides and mannitol), glycerine, titanium dioxide (white colour), medium chain triglycerides, lecithin and black ink containing: propylene glycol, black iron oxide, polyvinyl acetate phthalate, polyethylene glycol 400 and ammonium hydroxide.
