# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Norvir oral solution is supplied in amber coloured multiple-dose polyethylene terephthalate (PET) bottles in a 90 ml size.
Each commercial pack contains 5 bottles of 90 ml (450ml).
A dosage cup containing graduations at 3.75 ml (300 mg dose), 5 ml (400 mg dose), 6.25 ml (500 mg dose) and 7.5 ml (600mg dose) is provided.

[Norvir capsule 100 mg]

Norvir capsules are supplied in amber coloured high density polyethylene (HDPE) bottles containing 84 capsules.
Each commercial pack contains 4 bottles of 84 capsules (336 capsules).
