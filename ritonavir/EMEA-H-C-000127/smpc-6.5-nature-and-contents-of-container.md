# 6.5 Nature and content of container

Norvir oral solution is supplied in amber coloured multiple-dose polyethylene terephthalate (PET) bottles in a 90 ml size.
Each commercial pack contains 5 bottles of 90 ml (450 ml).
A dosage cup containing graduations at 3.75 ml (300 mg dose), 5 ml (400 mg dose), 6.25 ml (500 mg dose) and 7.5 ml (600 mg dose) is provided.
