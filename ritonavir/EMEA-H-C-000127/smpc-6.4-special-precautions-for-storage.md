# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Norvir oral solution should be stored under refrigeration between 2º-8ºC until it is dispensed to the patient.
Refrigeration by the patient is not required if used within 30 days and stored below 30°C.

Avoid exposure to excessive heat.

[Norvir capsule 100 mg]

Norvir capsules should be stored under refrigeration between 2º-8ºC at all times.

Avoid exposure to freezing and excessive heat.
