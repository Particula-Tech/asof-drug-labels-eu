# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Vemlidy tablets are packaged in high density polyethylene (HDPE) bottles and enclosed with a polypropylene continuous-thread, child-resistant cap, lined with an induction-activated aluminium foil liner.
Each bottle contains silica gel dessicant and polyester coil.

The following pack sizes are available: outer cartons containing 1 bottle of 30 film-coated tablets and outer cartons containing 90 (3 bottles of 30) film-coated tablets.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.
