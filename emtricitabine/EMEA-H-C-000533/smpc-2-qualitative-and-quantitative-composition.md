# 2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION

Each hard capsule contains 200 mg of emtricitabine.

For the full list of excipients, see section 6.1.

[Emtriva 10 mg/ml oral solution]

Each ml of Emtriva oral solution contains 10 mg of emtricitabine.

Excipient(s) with known effect:

Each dose (24 ml) contains 36 mg methyl parahydroxybenzoate (E218), 3.6 mg propyl parahydroxybenzoate (E216), 1.2 mg Sunset Yellow (E110) and has a sodium content of 36 mg.
