# 4.9 Overdose

Because Lucentis is administered by qualified ophthalmologists experienced in intravitreal injections, the likelihood of an overdose is very low.
Only two cases of accidental overdose have been reported from the clinical studies.
One patient received 1.2 mg Lucentis instead of the randomised dose
(0.3 mg) while the second patient was treated with 2.0 mg instead of 0.5 mg.
No adverse events were associated with these overdoses except for mild and transient increases in intraocular pressure.
If an overdose occurs, intraocular pressure should be monitored and treated, if deemed necessary by the attending physician.
