# 4.9 Overdose

Cases of accidental overdose have been reported from the clinical studies in wet AMD and post-marketing data.
Adverse reactions associated with these reported cases were intraocular pressure increased, transient blindness, reduced visual acuity, corneal oedema, corneal pain, and eye pain.
If an overdose occurs, intraocular pressure should be monitored and treated, if deemed necessary by the attending physician.
