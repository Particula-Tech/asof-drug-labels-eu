# 6.6 Special precautions for disposal and other handling

Siklos is a medicinal product that must be handled with care.
People who are not taking Siklos and in particular pregnant women should avoid being in contact with hydroxycarbamide.

In case the prescribed dosage requires breaking the tablet in halves or quarters, this should be done out of the reach of food.
Powder eventually spilled from the broken tablet should be wiped up with a damp disposable towel, which is discarded.
Unused broken tablets must be replaced in the blister and put back in the box.
Anyone handling Siklos should wash ones hands before and after contact with the tablets.

Any unused product or waste material should be disposed of in the accordance with local requirements.
