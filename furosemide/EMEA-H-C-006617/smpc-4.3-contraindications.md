# 4.3 Contraindications

- Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients listed in section 6.1.
- Anuria or acute renal failure with anuria not responding to furosemide.
- Hepatic encephalopathy.
- Urinary tract obstruction.
- Hypovolaemia.
- Dehydration.
- Severe hypokalaemia.
- Severe hyponatraemia.
- Progressive hepatitis and severe hepatocellular insufficiency in patients on haemodialysis and in patients with severe renal failure (creatinine clearance less than 30 mL/min) due to the risk of accumulation of furosemide which is mainly excreted via the biliary route in this case.
