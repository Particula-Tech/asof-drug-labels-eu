# 4.9 Overdose

Experience with doses greater than 800 mg is limited.
In the event of overdosage, the patient should be observed and appropriate supportive treatment given.

A patient with myeloid blast crisis inadvertently took Glivec 1200 mg for 6 days and experienced Grade 1 elevations of serum creatinine, Grade 2 ascites and elevated liver transaminase levels, and Grade 3 elevations of bilirubin.
Therapy was temporarily interrupted and there was complete reversal of all abnormalities within one week.
Treatment was resumed at a dose of 400 mg without recurrence of problems.
