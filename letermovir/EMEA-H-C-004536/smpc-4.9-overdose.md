# 4.9 Overdose

There is no experience with human overdose with PREVYMIS.
During Phase 1 clinical trials, 86 healthy subjects received doses ranging from 720 mg/day to 1440 mg/day of PREVYMIS for up to 14 days.
The adverse reaction profile was similar to that of the clinical dose of 480 mg/day.
There is no specific antidote for overdose with PREVYMIS.
In case of overdose, it is recommended that the patient be monitored for adverse reactions and appropriate symptomatic treatment instituted.

It is unknown whether dialysis will result in meaningful removal of PREVYMIS from systemic circulation.
