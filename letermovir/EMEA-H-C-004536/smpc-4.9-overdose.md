# 4.9 Overdose

There is no experience with human overdose with letermovir.
During Phase 1 clinical trials, 86 healthy adult subjects received doses ranging from 720 mg/day to 1 440 mg/day of letermovir for up to 14 days.
The adverse reaction profile was similar to that of the clinical dose of 480 mg/day.
There is no specific antidote for overdose with letermovir.
In case of overdose, it is recommended that the patient be monitored for adverse reactions and appropriate symptomatic treatment instituted.

It is unknown whether dialysis will result in meaningful removal of letermovir from systemic circulation.
