# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

ISENTRESS is indicated in combination with other anti-retroviral medicinal products for the treatment of human immunodeficiency virus (HIV-1) infection in adult patients.

This indication is based on safety and efficacy data from two double-blind, placebo-controlled trials of 48 weeks duration in treatment-experienced patients and one double-blind, active-controlled trial of 48 weeks duration in treatment-naïve patients (see sections 4.4 and 5.1).
