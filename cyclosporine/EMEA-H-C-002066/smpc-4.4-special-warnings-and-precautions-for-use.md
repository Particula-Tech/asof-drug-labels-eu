# 4.4 Special warnings and precautions for use

IKERVIS has not been studied in patients with a history of ocular herpes and should therefore be used with caution in such patients.

Contact lenses
Patients wearing contact lenses have not been studied.
Careful monitoring of patients with severe keratitis is recommended.
Contact lenses should be removed before instillation of the eye drops at bedtime and may be reinserted at wake-up time.

Concomitant therapy
There is limited experience with ciclosporin in the treatment of patients with glaucoma.
Regular clinical monitoring should be exercised when treating these patients concomitantly with IKERVIS, especially with beta-blockers which are known to decrease tear secretion.

Effects on the immune system
Ophthalmic medicinal products, which affect the immune system, including ciclosporin, may affect host defences against local infections and malignancies.
Therefore, regular examination of the eye(s) is recommended, e.g. at least every 6 months, when IKERVIS is used for years.

Cetalkonium chloride content
IKERVIS contains cetalkonium chloride.
Contact lenses should be removed prior to application and may be reinserted at wake-up time.
Cetalkonium chloride may cause eye irritation.
Patients should be monitored in case of prolonged use.
