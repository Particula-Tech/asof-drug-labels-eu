# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

IKERVIS is supplied in 0.3 mL single-dose, low-density polyethylene (LDPE) containers presented in a sealed laminate aluminium pouch.
One pouch contains five single-dose containers.

Pack sizes: 30 and 90 single-dose containers.
Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[IKERVIS 1 mg/mL eye drops, emulsion (SmPC 2)]

IKERVIS is supplied sterile in a white low density polyethylene bottle and white nozzle with tamper evident system.

The following pack sizes are available: Carton containing 1 bottle of 5 mL with 2.5 mL fill, carton containing 1 bottle of 11 mL with 4.5 mL fill or carton containing 1 bottle of 11 mL with 7 mL fill.
