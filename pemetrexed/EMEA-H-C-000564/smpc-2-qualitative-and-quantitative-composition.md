# 2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION

Each vial contains 500 mg of pemetrexed (as pemetrexed disodium).

Each vial must be reconstituted with 20 ml of sodium chloride 9 mg/ml (0.9 %) solution for injection resulting in 25 mg/ml of solution.
The appropriate volume of required dose is removed from the vial and further diluted to 100 ml with sodium chloride 9 mg/ml (0.9 %) solution for injection (see section 6.6.).
