# 4.9 Overdose

Symptoms
If overdose occurs the patient must be monitored for evidence of toxicity (see sections 4.8 and 5.3), and standard supportive treatment applied as necessary.

Management
Tenofovir can be removed by haemodialysis; the median haemodialysis clearance of tenofovir is 134 ml/min.
It is not known whether tenofovir can be removed by peritoneal dialysis.
