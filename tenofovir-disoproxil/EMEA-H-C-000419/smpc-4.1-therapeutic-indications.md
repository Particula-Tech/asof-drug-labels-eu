# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

Viread is indicated in combination with other antiretroviral medicinal products for the treatment of HIV-1 infected adults over 18 years of age.

The demonstration of benefit of Viread is based on results of one study in treatment-naïve patients, including patients with a high viral load (> 100,000 copies/ml) and studies in which Viread was added to stable background therapy (mainly tritherapy) in antiretroviral pre-treated patients experiencing early virological failure (< 10,000 copies/ml, with the majority of patients having < 5,000 copies/ml).

The choice of Viread to treat antiretroviral experienced patients should be based on individual viral resistance testing and/or treatment history of patients.
