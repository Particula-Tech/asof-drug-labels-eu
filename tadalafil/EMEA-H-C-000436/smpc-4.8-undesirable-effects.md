# 4.8 Undesirable effects

The most commonly reported adverse reactions were headache and dyspepsia.
The adverse reactions reported were transient, and generally mild or moderate.
Adverse reaction data are limited in patients over 75 years of age.

The table below lists the adverse reactions reported during placebo-controlled clinical trials for registration in patients treated with CIALIS on demand and daily dosing.
Adverse reactions are also included that have been reported from postmarketing surveillance in patients taking CIALIS on demand.
