# 4.6 Fertility, pregnancy and lactation

CIALIS is not indicated for use by women.

Pregnancy

There are limited data from the use of tadalafil in pregnant women.
Animal studies do not indicate direct or indirect harmful effects with respect to pregnancy, embryonal/foetal development, parturition or postnatal development (see section 5.3).
As a precautionary measure, it is preferable to avoid the use of CIALIS during pregnancy.

Breastfeeding

Available pharmacodynamic/toxicological data in animals have shown excretion of tadalafil in milk.
A risk to the suckling child cannot be excluded.
CIALIS should not be used during breast feeding.

Fertility

Effects were seen in dogs that might indicate impairment of fertility.
Two subsequent clinical studies suggest that this effect is unlikely in humans, although a decrease in sperm concentration was seen in some men (see sections 5.1 and 5.3).
