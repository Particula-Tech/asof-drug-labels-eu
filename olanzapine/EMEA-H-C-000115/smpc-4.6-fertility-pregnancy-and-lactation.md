# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

Pregnancy: There are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women.
Patients should be advised to notify their physician if they become pregnant or intend to become pregnant during treatment with olanzapine.
Nevertheless, because human experience is limited, this drug should be used in pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus.

Lactation: Olanzapine was excreted in milk of treated rats during lactation.
It is not known if olanzapine is excreted in human milk.
Patients should be advised not to breast feed an infant if they are taking olanzapine.
