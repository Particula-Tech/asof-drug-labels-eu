[ZYPREXA 10 mg powder for solution for injection.]

Reconstitute ZYPREXA only with Water for Injections using standard aseptic techniques for reconstitution of parenteral products.
No other solutions should be used for reconsititution.

1.
Withdraw 2.1 ml of Water for Injection into a sterile syringe.
Inject into a vial of ZYPREXA.

2.
Rotate the vial until the contents have completely dissolved, giving a yellow coloured solution.
The vial contains 11.0 mg olanzapine as a solution of 5 mg/ml (1 mg olanzapine is retained in the vial and syringe, thus allowing delivery of 10 mg olanzapine).

3.
The following table provides injection volumes for delivering various doses of olanzapine:

| Dose (mg) | Volume of injection (ml) |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | 2.0 |
| 7.5 | 1.5 |
| 5 | 1.0 |

4.
Administer the solution intramuscularly.
Do not administer intravenously or subcutaneously.

5.
Discard the syringe and any unused solution in accordance with appropriate clinical procedures.
6.
Use the solution immediately within 1 hour of reconstitution.
Do not store above 25º C. Do not freeze.

Parenteral medicines should be inspected visually for particulate matter prior to administration.

[ZYPREXA 10 mg powder and solvent for solution for injection.]

Reconstitute ZYPREXA only with the solvent provided in the pack using standard aseptic techniques for reconstitution of parenteral products.
No other solvents should be used for reconstitution.

The syringe includes an integral needle safety guard that is activated after administration of ZYPREXA.
Do not slide the guard during reconstitution and administration, as this will prematurely engage the guard.
This is irreversible and prevents the use of the syringe and needle.

Inject the entire contents of the prefilled syringe into the vial of ZYPREXA powder.

The vial contains 11.0 mg olanzapine as a solution of 10 mg/ml (1 mg olanzapine is retained in the vial and syringe, thus allowing delivery of 10 mg olanzapine).

Draw the solution into the syringe up to the mark showing the required dose.
The syringe is marked showing doses of 2.5, 5, 7.5 and 10 mg olanzapine.

Hold the needle safety guard and pull back on the syringe.
A click confirms that the guard is correctly engaged.
Ideally, do this at the same time as withdrawing the needle from the muscle.
