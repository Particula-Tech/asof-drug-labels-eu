# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

ZYPREXA 10 mg Powder for Solution for Injection is available in a Type I glass vial.
One carton contains 1 or 10 vial(s).

[ZYPREXA 10 mg Powder and Solvent for Solution for Injection.]

ZYPREXA 10 mg Powder and Solvent for Solution for Injection comprises one each of the following:

- ZYPREXA 10 mg Powder for Solution for Injection: Type I glass vial.
- Sodium Chloride Solution Solvent for Parenteral Use, 1 ml: 1 prefilled Type I glass syringe with a 22 gauge needle.
  The syringe includes an integral needle safety guard.
