# 6.1 List of excipients

Inactive ingredients are carnauba wax, color mixture white (titanium dioxide E171, macrogol, polysorbate 80), crospovidone, edible blue ink (contains indigo carmine colour E132), hydroxypropyl cellulose, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, methylhydroxypropylcellulose, microcrystalline cellulose.
