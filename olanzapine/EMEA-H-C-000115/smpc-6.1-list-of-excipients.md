# 6.1 List of excipients

Inactive ingredients are carnauba wax, colour mixture light blue (titanium dioxide (E171), lactose monohydrate, hypromellose, triacetin and indigo carmine colour (E132)), crospovidone, hydroxypropylcellulose, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, hypromellose, microcrystalline cellulose.

[ZYPREXA 20 mg coated tablets.]

Inactive ingredients are carnauba wax, colour mixture pink (titanium dioxide (E171), macrogol, lactose monohydrate, hypromellose and synthetic red iron oxide), crospovidone, hydroxypropyl cellulose, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, hypromellose, microcrystalline cellulose.
