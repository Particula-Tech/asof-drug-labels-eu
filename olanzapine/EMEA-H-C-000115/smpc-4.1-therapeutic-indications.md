# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

Olanzapine is indicated for the treatment of schizophrenia.
Olanzapine is effective in maintaining the clinical improvement during continuation therapy in patients who have shown an initial treatment response.

4.1.2 Further information on clinical trials

In a multinational, double-blind, comparative study of schizophrenia, schizoaffective, and related disorders which included 1481 patients with varying degrees of associated depressive symptoms (baseline mean of 16.6 on the Montgomery-Asberg Depression Rating Scale), a prospective secondary analysis of baseline to endpoint mood score change demonstrated a statistically significant improvement (p=0.001) favoring olanzapine (-6.0) versus haloperidol (-3.1).
