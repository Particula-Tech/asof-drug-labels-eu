# 4.9 Overdose

Experience in adults exposed to doses of up to 900 mg/day for 8 weeks revealed no toxicity.
The most likely manifestations of overdosage are expected to be hypotension and tachycardia; bradycardia might also occur from overdose.
No specific information is available on the treatment of overdosage with Aprovel.
The patient should be closely monitored, and the treatment should be symptomatic and supportive.
Suggested measures include induction of emesis and/or gastric lavage.
Activated charcoal may be useful in the treatment of overdosage.
Irbesartan is not removed by haemodialysis.
