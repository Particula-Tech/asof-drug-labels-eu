# 4.3 Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients listed in section 6.1.
Second and third trimesters of pregnancy (see sections 4.4 and 4.6).

Do not co-administer Aprovel with aliskiren-containing medicines in patients with diabetes or with moderate to severe renal impairment (glomerular filtration rate (GFR) <60 ml/min/1.73 m²) (see sections 4.4 and 4.5).
