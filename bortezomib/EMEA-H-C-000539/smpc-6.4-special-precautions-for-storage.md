# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Do not store above 30°C.
Keep the container in the outer carton in order to protect from light.

The reconstituted solution should be used immediately after preparation.
If the reconstituted solution is not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user.
However, the chemical and physical in-use stability of the reconstituted solution has been demonstrated for 8 hours at 25 °C stored in the original vial and/or a syringe prior to administration, with a maximum of 3 hours in the syringe.
