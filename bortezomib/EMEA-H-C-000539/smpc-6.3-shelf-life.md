# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened vial
3 years

Reconstituted solution
The reconstituted solution should be used immediately after preparation.
If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user.
However, the chemical and physical in-use stability of the reconstituted solution has been demonstrated for 8 hours at 25°C stored in the original vial and/or a syringe.
The total storage time for the reconstituted medicinal product should not exceed 8 hours prior to administration.
