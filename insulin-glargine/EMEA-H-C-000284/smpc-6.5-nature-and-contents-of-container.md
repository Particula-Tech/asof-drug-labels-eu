# 6.5 Nature and content of container

5 ml, type 1 colourless glass vial with flanged aluminium cap, chlorobutyl rubber (type 1) stopper and tear-off polypropylene lid.
Each vial contains 5 ml solution (500 IU insulin glargine).
Packs of 1, 2, 5 and 10 vials are available.

10 ml, type 1 colourless glass vial with flanged aluminium cap, rubber (type 1, laminate of polyisoprene and bromobutyl) stopper and tear-off polypropylene lid.
Each vial contains 10 ml solution (1000 IU insulin glargine).
Packs of 1 vial are available.

[Lantus 100 IU/ml solution for injection in a cartridge.]

3 ml, type 1 colourless glass cartridge with black bromobutyl rubber plunger and flanged aluminium cap with black bromobutyl rubber stopper.
Each cartridge contains 3 ml solution (300 IU insulin glargine).
Packs of 4, 5 and 10 cartridges are available.

[Lantus 100 IU/ml OptiSet solution for injection]

The cartridges are sealed in a disposable pen injector.
Needles are not included in the pack.
Packs of 3, 4, 5 and 10 pens are available.
