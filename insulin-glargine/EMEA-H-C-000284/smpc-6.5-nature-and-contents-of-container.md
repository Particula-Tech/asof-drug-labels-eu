# 6.5 Nature and content of container

5 ml, type 1 colourless glass vial with flanged aluminium cap, chlorobutyl rubber (type 1) stopper and tear-off polypropylene lid.
Each vial contains 5 ml solution (500 Units insulin glargine).
Packs of 1, 2, 5 and 10 vials are available.

10 ml, type 1 colourless glass vial with flanged aluminium cap, rubber (type 1, laminate of polyisoprene and bromobutyl) stopper and tear-off polypropylene lid.
Each vial contains 10 ml solution (1000 Units insulin glargine).
Packs of 1 vial are available.

[Lantus 100 Units/ml solution for injection in a cartridge.]

3 ml, type 1 colourless glass cartridge with black bromobutyl rubber plunger and flanged aluminium cap with bromobutyl or laminate of polyisoprene and bromobutyl rubber stopper.
Each cartridge contains 3 ml solution (300 Units insulin glargine).
Packs of 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 10 cartridges are available.
Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[Lantus 100 Units/ml solution for injection in a cartridge. (SmPC 3)]

The glass cartridge is irreversibly integrated in a transparent container and assembled to a plastic mechanism with a threaded rod at one extremity
Packs of 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 10 cartridges for OptiClik are available.

[Lantus 100 Units/ml OptiSet, solution for injection in a pre-filled pen.]

The cartridges are sealed in a disposable pen injector.
Needles are not included in the pack.
Packs of 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 10 pens are available.
