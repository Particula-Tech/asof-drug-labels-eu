# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

3 ml, type 1 colourless glass cartridge with black bromobutyl rubber plunger and flanged aluminium cap with black bromobutyl rubber stopper.
Each cartridge contains 3 ml solution (300 IU insulin glargine).
The cartridges are sealed in a disposable pen injector.
Needles are not included in the pack.
Packs of 3, 4, 5 and 10 pens are available.
