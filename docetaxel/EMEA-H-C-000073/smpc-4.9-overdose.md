# 4.9 OVERDOSE

There were two reports of overdose.
One patient received 150 mg/m² and the other received 200 mg/m² of docetaxel as a one hour infusion.
They both recovered after experiencing severe neutropenia, mild asthenia, cutaneous reactions and mild paresthesia.

In case of overdose, the patient should be kept in a specialised unit and vital functions closely monitored.
There is no known antidote for docetaxel overdose.
The primary anticipated complications of overdose would consist of bone marrow suppression, peripheral neurotoxicity and mucositis.
