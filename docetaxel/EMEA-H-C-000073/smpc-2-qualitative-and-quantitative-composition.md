# 2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION

Single-dose vials of TAXOTERE 20 mg concentrate for solution for infusion contain docetaxel which is a trihydrate corresponding to 20 mg of docetaxel (anhydrous).
The viscous solution contains 40 mg/ml docetaxel (anhydrous).

For excipients, see section 6.1.

[TAXOTERE 80 mg concentrate and solvent for solution for infusion]

Single-dose vials of TAXOTERE 80 mg concentrate for solution for infusion contain docetaxel which is a trihydrate corresponding to 80 mg of docetaxel (anhydrous).
