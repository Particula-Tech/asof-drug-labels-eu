# 2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION

Each single-dose vial of TAXOTERE 20 mg/0.5 ml concentrate contains docetaxel (as trihydrate) corresponding to 20 mg of docetaxel (anhydrous).
The viscous solution contains 40 mg/ml docetaxel (anhydrous).

Excipients with known effect:
Each single-dose vial of solvent contains 13% (w/w) ethanol 95% v/v in water for injection (252 mg of ethanol 95% v/v).

For the full list of excipients, see section 6.1.

[TAXOTERE 80 mg/2 ml concentrate and solvent for solution for infusion]

Each single-dose vial of TAXOTERE 80 mg/2 ml concentrate contains docetaxel (as trihydrate) corresponding to 80 mg of docetaxel (anhydrous).

Excipient with known effect:
Each single-dose vial of solvent contains 13% (w/w) ethanol 95% v/v in water for injection (932 mg of ethanol 95% v/v).

[TAXOTERE 20 mg/1 ml concentrate for solution for infusion]

Each ml of concentrate contains 20 mg docetaxel (as trihydrate).

One vial of 1 ml of concentrate contains 20 mg of docetaxel.

Each vial of concentrate contains 0.5 ml of ethanol anhydrous (395 mg).

[TAXOTERE 80 mg/4 ml concentrate for solution for infusion]

One vial of 4 ml of concentrate contains 80 mg of docetaxel.

Each vial of concentrate contains 2 ml of ethanol anhydrous (1.58 g).

[TAXOTERE 160 mg/8 ml concentrate for solution for infusion.]

One vial of 8 ml of concentrate contains 160 mg of docetaxel.

Each vial of concentrate contains 4 ml of ethanol anhydrous (3.16 g).
