# 6.3 SHELF-LIFE

Vials of TAXOTERE® concentrate for infusion should be stored under refrigeration and protected from bright light.
The shelf-life under these conditions is 12 months for TAXOTERE ® 20 mg.
Freezing does not adversely affect the product.

[TAXOTERE® (docetaxel) 80 mg]

The shelf-life under these conditions is 15 months for TAXOTERE ® 80 mg.
