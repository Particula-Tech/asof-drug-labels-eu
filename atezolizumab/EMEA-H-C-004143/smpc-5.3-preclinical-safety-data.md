# 5.3 Preclinical safety data

Carcinogenicity

Carcinogenicity studies have not been performed to establish the carcinogenic potential of atezolizumab.

Mutagenicity

Mutagenicity studies have not been performed to establish the mutagenic potential of atezolizumab.
However, monoclonal antibodies are not expected to alter DNA or chromosomes.

Fertility

No fertility studies have been conducted with atezolizumab; however assessment of the cynomolgus monkey male and female reproductive organs was included in the chronic toxicity study.
Weekly administration of atezolizumab to female monkeys at an estimated AUC approximately 6 times the AUC in patients receiving the recommended dose caused an irregular menstrual cycle pattern and a lack of newly formed corpora lutea in the ovaries which were reversible.
There was no effect on the male reproductive organs.
Teratogenicity

No reproductive or teratogenicity studies in animals have been conducted with atezolizumab.
Animal studies have demonstrated that inhibition of the PD-L1/PD-1 pathway can lead to immune-related rejection of the developing foetus resulting in foetal death.
Administration of atezolizumab could cause foetal harm, including embryo-foetal lethality.
