# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened vial

3 years.

Diluted solution

Chemical and physical in-use stability has been demonstrated for up to 24 hours at ≤ 30 °C and for up to 30 days at 2 °C to 8 °C from the time of preparation.

From a microbiological point of view, the prepared solution for infusion should be used immediately.
If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and would normally not be longer than 24 hours at 2 °C to 8 °C or 8 hours at ambient temperature (≤ 25 °C) unless dilution has taken place in controlled and validated aseptic conditions.
