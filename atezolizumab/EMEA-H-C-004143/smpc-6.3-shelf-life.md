# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened vial

2 years.

Diluted solution

Chemical and physical in-use stability has been demonstrated for no more than 24 hours at 2 °C to 8 °C or 8 hours at ambient temperature (≤ 30 °C) from the time of preparation.

From a microbiological point of view, the prepared solution for infusion should be used immediately.
If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user.
