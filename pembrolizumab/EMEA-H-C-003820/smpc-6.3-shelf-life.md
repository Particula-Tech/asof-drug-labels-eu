# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened vial
2 years

After reconstitution
Chemical and physical in-use stability of the reconstituted and diluted solution has been demonstrated for 24 hours at room temperatures (at or below 25°C).
From a microbiological point of view, the product must be used immediately.
Do not freeze the reconstituted or diluted solution.
If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and must not be longer than a total of 24 hours.
This 24 hour hold may include up to 6 hours at room temperatures (at or below 25°C); any additional hold time must be at 2°C-8°C.
If refrigerated, allow the vials and/or intravenous bags to come to room temperature prior to use.
