# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened vial
2 years.

After preparation of infusion
Chemical and physical in-use stability has been demonstrated for up to 42 days at 2 °C to 8 °C or at 23 °C to 27 °C.
From a microbiological point of view, the product, once diluted, should be used immediately.
The diluted solution must not be frozen.
If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and would normally not be longer than 7 days at 2 °C to 8 °C, or 12 hours at room temperature, unless dilution has taken place in controlled and validated aseptic conditions.
If refrigerated, the vials and/or intravenous bags must be allowed to come to room temperature prior to use.
