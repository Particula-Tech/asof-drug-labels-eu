# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

High density polyethylene bottles, containing 60 or 200 capsules, with a desiccant canister and polyester coil, and a child resistant polypropylene cap with a polystyrene foam inner seal.

Package sizes are: 1 x 60 capsules

1 x 200 capsules
4 x 200 capsules

6 x 200 capsules
