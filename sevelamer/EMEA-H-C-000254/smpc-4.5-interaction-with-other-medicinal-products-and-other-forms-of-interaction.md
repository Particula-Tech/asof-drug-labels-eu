# 4.5 Interactions with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

Interaction studies have not been conducted in patients on haemodialysis.

In randomised, crossover interaction studies performed in healthy volunteers the pharmacokinetic profiles of four medicinal products commonly used in the target patient population (digoxin, warfarin, enalapril and metoprolol, single oral dose) did not show any statistically significant difference, comparing the drug alone to the drug in combination with Renagel.
In animal studies, co-administration of a single dose of Renagel with verapamil, quinidine, calcitriol, tetracycline, warfarin, valproic acid, digoxin, propranolol, estrone and L-thyroxin did not alter peak serum concentrations or area under the curve for serum concentrations of these products.
