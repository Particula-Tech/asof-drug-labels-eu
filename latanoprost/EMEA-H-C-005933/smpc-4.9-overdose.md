# 4.9 Overdose

Overdose is unlikely to occur after ocular administration.
If overdose occurs, treatment should be symptomatic.

Symptoms

Apart from ocular irritation and conjunctival hyperaemia, no other ocular side effects are known if latanoprost is overdosed via the ocular route.

Treatment

If overdosage with this medicine occurs, treatment should be symptomatic.

Paediatric population

The principles described above apply to the management of overdose in the paediatric population.
