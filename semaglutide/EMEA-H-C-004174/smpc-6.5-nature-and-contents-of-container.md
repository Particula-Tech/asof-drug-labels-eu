# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

1.5 ml glass cartridge (type I glass) closed at the one end with a rubber plunger (chlorobutyl) and at the other end with an aluminium cap with a laminated rubber sheet (bromobutyl/polyisoprene) inserted.
The cartridge is assembled into a multidose disposable pre-filled pen made of polypropylene, polyoxymethylene, polycarbonate and acrylonitrile butadiene styrene.

Each pen contains 1.5 ml solution and is able to deliver doses of 0.25 mg, 0.5 mg and 1 mg.

Pack size
1 pre-filled pen and 6 disposable NovoFine Plus needles
Not all pack sizes may be marketed.
