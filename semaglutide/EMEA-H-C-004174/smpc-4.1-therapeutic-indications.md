# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

Ozempic is indicated for the treatment of adults with insufficiently controlled type 2 diabetes mellitus as an adjunct to diet and exercise

- as monotherapy when metformin is considered inappropriate due to intolerance or contraindications
- in addition to other medicinal products for the treatment of diabetes.

For trial results with respect to combinations, effects on glycaemic control, cardiovascular disease and kidney events and the populations studied, see sections 4.4, 4.5 and 5.1.
