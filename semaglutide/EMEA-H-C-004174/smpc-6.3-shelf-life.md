# 6.3 Shelf life

Before first use

Ozempic 0.25 mg, 0.5 mg, 1 mg and 2 mg
3 years
After first opening

In-use shelf life

Ozempic 0.25 mg, 0.5 mg, 1 mg and 2 mg (4 dose pens)
6 weeks

Ozempic 0.5 mg and 1 mg (8 dose pens)
8 weeks

Store below 30 °C or in a refrigerator (2 °C–8 °C).
Do not freeze Ozempic.
Keep the pen cap on when the pen is not in use in order to protect it from light.
