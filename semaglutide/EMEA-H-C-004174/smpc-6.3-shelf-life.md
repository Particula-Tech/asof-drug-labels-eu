# 6.3 Shelf life

Pre-filled pen

Before first use

3 years

After first opening

In-use shelf life

Ozempic 0.25 mg, 0.5 mg, 1 mg and 2 mg (4 dose pens)
6 weeks

Ozempic 0.5 mg and 1 mg (8 dose pens)
8 weeks

Store below 30 °C or in a refrigerator (2 °C–8 °C).
Do not freeze Ozempic.
Keep the pen cap on when the pen is not in use in order to protect it from light.

Pre-filled syringe
2 years

Ozempic may be stored unrefrigerated for up to 28 days at a temperature not above 30 °C.
Discard the syringe if it has been out of the refrigerator for more than 28 days.
