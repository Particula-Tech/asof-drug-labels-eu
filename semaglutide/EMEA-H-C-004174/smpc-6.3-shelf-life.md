# 6.3 Shelf life

Before first use

Ozempic 0.25 mg, 0.5 mg and 1 mg
3 years.

Ozempic 2 mg
2 years.

After first opening

In-use shelf life: 6 weeks.

Store below 30 °C or in a refrigerator (2 °C–8 °C).
Do not freeze Ozempic and do not use Ozempic if it has been frozen.
Keep the pen cap on when the pen is not in use in order to protect it from light.

Always remove the injection needle after each injection and store the pen without a needle attached.
This may prevent blocked needles, contamination, infection, leakage of solution and inaccurate dosing.
