# 4.9 Overdose

Cases of overdose (2-3 times the prescribed dose for several weeks) have been reported.
In one case, this resulted in subclinical hepatitis which resolved after a dose interruption.
Single doses of 80 mg/kg of the deferasirox dispersible tablet formulation in iron-overloaded thalassaemic patients caused mild nausea and diarrhoea.

Acute signs of overdose may include nausea, vomiting, headache and diarrhoea.
Overdose may be treated by induction of emesis or by gastric lavage, and by symptomatic treatment.

[EXJADE 90 mg film-coated tablets]

Single doses of 80 mg/kg of the deferasirox dispersible tablet formulation (corresponding to a dose of 56 mg/kg film-coated tablets) in iron-overloaded thalassaemic patients caused mild nausea and diarrhoea.

[EXJADE 90 mg granules in sachet]

Single doses of 80 mg/kg of the deferasirox dispersible tablet formulation (corresponding to a dose of 56 mg/kg granules) in iron-overloaded thalassaemic patients caused mild nausea and diarrhoea.
