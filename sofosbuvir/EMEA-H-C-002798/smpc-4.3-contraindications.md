# 4.3 Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients listed in section 6.1.

Medicinal products that are potent P-glycoprotein (P-gp) inducers in the intestine (rifampicin, rifabutin, St. John’s wort [Hypericum perforatum], carbamazepine, phenobarbital and phenytoin).
Co-administration will significantly decrease sofosbuvir plasma concentration and could result in loss of efficacy of Sovaldi (see section 4.5).
