# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

A specific study to assess the possible influence of ivabradine on driving performance has been performed in healthy volunteers where no alteration of the driving performance was evidenced.
However, in post-marketing experience, cases of impaired driving ability due to visual symptoms have been reported.
Ivabradine may cause transient luminous phenomena consisting mainly of phosphenes (see section 4.8).
The possible occurrence of such luminous phenomena should be taken into account when driving or using machines in situations where sudden variations in light intensity may occur, especially when driving at night.
Ivabradine has no influence on the ability to use machines.
