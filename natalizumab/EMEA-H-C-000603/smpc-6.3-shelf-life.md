# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened vial

4 years

Diluted solution

After dilution with sodium chloride 9 mg/mL (0.9%) solution for injection, immediate use is recommended.
If not used immediately, the diluted solution must be stored at 2˚C to 8˚C and infused within 8 hours of dilution.
In-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user.

[Tysabri 150 mg solution for injection in pre-filled syringe]
