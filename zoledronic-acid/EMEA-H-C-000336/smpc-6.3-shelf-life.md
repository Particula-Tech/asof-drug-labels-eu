# 6.3 Shelf life

3 years.
The reconstituted solution is chemically and physically stable for 24 hours at room temperature.

[Zometa 4 mg/5 ml concentrate for solution for infusion]

The Zometa solution is stable for 24 hours at 2–8°C after further dilution in 100 ml physiological saline or 5% w/v glucose solution.
