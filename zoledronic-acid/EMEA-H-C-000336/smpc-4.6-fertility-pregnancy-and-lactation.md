# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

There are no adequate data on the use of zoledronic acid in pregnant women.
Animal reproduction studies with zoledronic acid have shown reproductive toxicity (see section 5.3, “Preclinical safety data”).
Dystocia was observed at the lowest dose (0.01 mg/kg bodyweight) tested in the rat.
The potential risk for humans is unknown.
Zometa should not be used during pregnancy.

It is not known whether zoledronic acid is excreted into human milk.
Zometa should not be used by breast-feeding women (see section 4.3, “Contraindications”).
