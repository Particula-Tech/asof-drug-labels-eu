# 4.3 Contraindications

Zometa powder for solution for infusion is contraindicated in pregnancy, breast-feeding women, in patients with clinically significant hypersensitivity to zoledronic acid, other bisphosphonates or any of the excipients in the formulation of Zometa.

[Zometa 4 mg/5 ml concentrate for solution for infusion]

Zometa concentrate is contraindicated in pregnancy, breast-feeding women, in patients with clinically significant hypersensitivity to zoledronic acid, other bisphosphonates or any of the excipients in the formulation of Zometa.
