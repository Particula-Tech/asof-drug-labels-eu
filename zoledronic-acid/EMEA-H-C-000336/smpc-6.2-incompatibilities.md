# 6.2 Incompatibilities

To avoid potential incompatibilities, Zometa reconstituted solution is to be diluted with 0.9 % w/v sodium chloride solution or 5 % w/v glucose solution.

Zometa reconstituted solution must not be mixed with calcium-containing solutions such as Ringer’s solution.

Studies with glass bottles, as well as several types of infusion bags and infusion lines made from polyvinylchloride, polyethylene and polypropylene (prefilled with 0.9 % w/v sodium chloride solution or 5 % w/v glucose solution), showed no incompatibility with Zometa.
