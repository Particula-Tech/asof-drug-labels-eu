# 4.2 Posology and method of administration

Zometa must only be used by clinicians experienced in the administration of intravenous bisphosphonates.

Adults and elderly
The recommended dose in hypercalcaemia (albumin-corrected serum calcium ≥ 12.0 mg/dl or 3.0 mmol/l) is 4 mg reconstituted and further diluted Zometa solution for infusion (diluted with 50 ml 0.9 % w/v sodium chloride or 5 % w/v glucose solution), given as a single 15-minute intravenous infusion.
Patients must be maintained well hydrated prior to and following administration of Zometa.

Renal impairment
No dose adjustment is necessary in patients with mild to moderate renal impairment (serum creatinine < 400 µmol/l or < 4.5 mg/dl).

Retreatment
Limited data are available regarding retreatment (see section 5.1).

For information on the reconstitution and dilution of Zometa, see section 6.6, “Instructions for use and handling”.
