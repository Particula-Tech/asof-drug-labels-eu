# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Kalydeco tablets are packaged in a thermoform (polychlorotrifluoroethylene (PCTFE)/foil) blister or a high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottle with a polypropylene, foil-lined induction seal closure and molecular sieve desiccant.

The following pack sizes are available:
− Blister pack containing 56 film-coated tablets
− Bottle containing 56 film-coated tablets

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.
