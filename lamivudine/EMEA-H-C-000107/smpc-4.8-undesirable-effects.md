# 4.8 Undesirable effects

Adverse events have been reported during therapy for HIV disease with Epivir alone and in combination with zidovudine.
With many it is unclear whether they are related to Epivir or are as a result of the underlying disease process.

The most commonly reported undesirable effects (occurring in more than 5% of patients) are shown in bold in the list below.

Gastrointestinal tract:- nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain or cramps, diarrhoea
Haematological:- Neutropenia and anaemia (both occasionally severe), thrombocytopenia Liver/pancreas:- transient rises in liver enzymes (AST, ALT), rises in serum amylase, pancreatitis Musculoskeletal:- arthralgia, muscle disorders including rarely rhabdomyolysis
Neurological:- headache, peripheral neuropathy or paraesthesiae
Respiratory tract:- cough, nasal symptoms
Skin:- rash, alopecia
Other:- fatigue, malaise, fever, insomnia

Cases of lactic acidosis, sometimes fatal, usually associated with severe hepatomegaly and hepatic steatosis, have been reported with the use of nucleoside analogues (see 4.4 Special warnings and special precautions for use).
