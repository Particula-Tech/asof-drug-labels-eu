# 6.5 Nature and content of container

Epivir tablets are available in child resistant HDPE bottles or PVC/aluminium foil blister packs each containing 60 tablets.

[Epivir 10 mg/ml oral solution]

Cartons containing 240 ml oral solution in a white high density polyethylene (HDPE) bottle, with a child resistant closure.
A 10 ml polypropylene oral dosing syringe and a polyethylene adapter are also included in the pack.

[Epivir 300 mg film-coated tablets]

Epivir tablets are available in child resistant HDPE bottles or PVC/aluminium foil blister packs each containing 30 tablets.
