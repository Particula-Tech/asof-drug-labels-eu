# 4.9 Overdose

Administration of lamivudine at very high dose levels in acute animal studies did not result in any organ toxicity.
No specific signs or symptoms have been identified following acute overdose with lamivudine, apart from those listed as undesirable effects.

If overdosage occurs the patient should be monitored, and standard supportive treatment applied as required.
Since lamivudine is dialysable, continuous haemodialysis could be used in the treatment of overdosage, although this has not been studied.
