# 4.7 EFFECTS ON ABILITY TO DRIVE AND USE MACHINES

There have been no studies to investigate the effect of lamivudine on driving performance or the ability to operate machinery.
Further, a detrimental effect on such activities cannot be predicted from the pharmacology of the drug.
Nevertheless, the clinical status of the patient and the adverse event profile of EPIVIR® should be borne in mind when considering the patient’s ability to drive or operate machinery.
