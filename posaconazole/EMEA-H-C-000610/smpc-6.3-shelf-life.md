# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened container: 3 years
After first opening the container: 4 weeks

[Noxafil 100 mg gastro-resistant tablets]

2 years

[Noxafil 300 mg concentrate for solution for infusion]

3 years

From a microbiological point of view, once admixed, the product should be used immediately.
If not used immediately, the solution can be stored up to 24 hours refrigerated 2°C-8°C.
This medicinal product is for single use only.

[Noxafil 300 mg gastro-resistant powder and solvent for oral suspension]

After reconstitution: 30 minutes.
