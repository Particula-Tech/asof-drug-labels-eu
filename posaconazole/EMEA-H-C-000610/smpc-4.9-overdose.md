# 4.9 Overdose

During clinical trials, patients who received posaconazole oral suspension doses up to 1,600 mg/day experienced no different adverse reactions from those reported with patients at the lower doses.
Accidental overdose was noted in one patient who took posaconazole oral suspension 1,200 mg twice a day for 3 days.
No adverse reactions were noted by the investigator.

Posaconazole is not removed by haemodialysis.
There is no special treatment available in the case of overdose with posaconazole.
Supportive care may be considered.

[Noxafil 100 mg gastro-resistant tablets]

There is no experience with overdose of posaconazole tablets.

[Noxafil 300 mg concentrate for solution for infusion]

There is no experience with overdose of posaconazole concentrate for solution for infusion.
