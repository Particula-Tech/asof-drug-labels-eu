# 4.9 Overdose

During clinical trials, patients who received posaconazole doses up to 1,600 mg/day experienced no different adverse reactions from those reported with patients at the lower doses.
Accidental overdose was noted in one patient who took 1,200 mg twice a day for 3 days.
No adverse reactions were noted by the investigator.

Posaconazole is not removed by haemodialysis.
