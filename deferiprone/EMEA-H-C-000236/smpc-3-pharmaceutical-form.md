# 3. PHARMACEUTICAL FORM

Film-coated tablet.

Ferriprox 500 mg film-coated tablets

White to off-white, capsule-shaped, film-coated tablet imprinted “APO” bisect “500” on one side, plain on the other.
The tablet is 7.1 mm x 17.5 mm x 6.8 mm and scored.
The tablet can be divided into equal halves.
