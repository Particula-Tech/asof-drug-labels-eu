# 4.9 Overdose

No cases of acute overdose have been reported.
However, neurological disorders (such as cerebellar symptoms, diplopia, lateral nystagmus, psychomotor slowdown, hand movements and axial hypotonia) have been observed in a nine year old patient who had been voluntarily prescribed for two years with three times the maximum recommended dose of 100 mg/kg/day.
The neurological disorders progressively regressed after deferiprone discontinuation.

In case of overdose, close clinical supervision of the patient is required.
