# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

The effect on pregnancy and lactation has not been investigated in humans.
Pramipexole was not teratogenic in rats and rabbits, but was embryotoxic in the rat at maternotoxic doses (see section 5.3).
SIFROL should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the foetus.

As SIFROL treatment inhibits secretion of prolactin in humans, inhibition of lactation is expected.
The excretion of SIFROL into breast milk has not been studied in women.
In rats, the concentration of drug-related radioactivity was higher in breast milk than in plasma.
In the absence of human data, SIFROL should not be used during breast-feeding, if possible.
However, if its use is unavoidable, breast-feeding should be discontinued.
