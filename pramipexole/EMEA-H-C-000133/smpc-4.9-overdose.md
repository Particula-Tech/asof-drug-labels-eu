# 4.9 Overdose

There is no clinical experience with massive overdosage.
The expected adverse events would be those related to the pharmacodynamic profile of a dopamine agonist, including nausea, vomiting, hyperkinesia, hallucinations, agitation and hypotension.
There is no established antidote for overdosage of a dopamine agonist.
If signs of central nervous system stimulation are present, a neuroleptic agent may be indicated.
Management of the overdose may require general supportive measures, along with gastric lavage, intravenous fluids, and electrocardiogram monitoring.
