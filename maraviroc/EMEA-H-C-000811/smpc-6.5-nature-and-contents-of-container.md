# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

CELSENTRI 25 mg film-coated tablets

High density polyethylene bottles (HDPE) with polypropylene child resistant (CR) closures and an aluminium foil/polyethylene heat induction seal containing 120 film-coated tablets.

CELSENTRI 75 mg film-coated tablets

High density polyethylene bottles (HDPE) with polypropylene child resistant (CR) closures and an aluminium foil/polyethylene heat induction seal containing 120 film-coated tablets.

CELSENTRI 150 mg film-coated tablets

High density polyethylene bottles (HDPE) with polypropylene child resistant (CR) closures and an aluminium foil/polyethylene heat induction seal containing 180 film-coated tablets.

Polyvinyl chloride (PVC) blisters with aluminium foil backing in a carton containing 30, 60, 90 film-coated tablets and multipacks containing 180 (2 packs of 90) film-coated.

CELSENTRI 300 mg film-coated tablets

High density polyethylene bottles (HDPE) with polypropylene child resistant (CR) closures and an aluminium foil/polyethylene heat induction seal containing 180 film-coated tablets.

Polyvinyl chloride (PVC) blisters with aluminium foil backing in a carton containing 30, 60, 90 film-coated tablets and multipacks containing 180 (2 packs of 90) film-coated tablets.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[CELSENTRI 20 mg/ml oral solution]

High density polyethylene (HDPE) bottle, with a child resistant closure, containing 230 mL maraviroc 20 mg/mL solution.
The pack also includes a thermoplastic elastomeric press in bottle adapter, and a 10 ml oral applicator comprised of a polypropylene barrel (with mL graduations) and a polyethylene plunger.

The oral applicator is provided for accurate measurement of the prescribed dose of oral solution.
