# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

This medicinal product does not require any special storage condition.

[CELSENTRI 20 mg/ml oral solution]

Store below 30 ºC.
Discard 60 days after first opening.
The discard date of the oral solution should be written on the carton in the space provided.
The date should be written as soon as the bottle has been opened for first use.
