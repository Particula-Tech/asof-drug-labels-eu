# 4.9 Overdose

There are no known specific signs and symptoms of overdose with efgartigimod alfa.
In the event of an overdose the adverse events that may occur are not expected to be different from those that may be observed at the recommended dose.
Patients should be monitored for adverse reactions, and appropriate symptomatic and supportive treatment initiated.
There is no specific antidote for overdose with efgartigimod alfa.
