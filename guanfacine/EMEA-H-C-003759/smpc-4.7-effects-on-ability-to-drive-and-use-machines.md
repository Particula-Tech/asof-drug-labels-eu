# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

Guanfacine may have a moderate to severe influence on the ability to drive and use machines.
Guanfacine can cause dizziness and somnolence.
These effects occur predominantly at the start of treatment and may occur less frequently as treatment continues.
Syncope has also been observed.
Patients should be warned of these possible effects and be advised that if affected, they should avoid these activities (see section 4.4).
