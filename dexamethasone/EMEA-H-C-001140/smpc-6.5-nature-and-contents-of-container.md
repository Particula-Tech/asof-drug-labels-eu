# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

1 pack contains:

1 sustained release sterile implantable rod shaped implant containing 700 micrograms of dexamethasone, located in the needle (stainless steel) of a disposable applicator.

The applicator consists of a plunger (stainless steel) within a needle where the implant is held in place by a sleeve (silicone).
The plunger is controlled by a lever on the side of the applicator body.
The needle is protected by a cap and the lever by a safety tab.
The applicator containing the implant is packaged in a sealed foil pouch containing desiccant.
