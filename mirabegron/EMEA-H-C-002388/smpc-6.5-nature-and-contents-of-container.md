# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Alu-Alu blisters in cartons containing 10, 20, 30, 50, 60, 90, 100 or 200 tablets.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[Betmiga 8 mg/ml granules for prolonged-release oral suspension]

There are two presentations of Betmiga granules for prolonged-release oral suspension, and both are supplied as several components for reconstitution.
One presentation contains:

- In the carton: pouch, 5 ml oral syringe, adaptor, and package leaflet
- In the pouch: measuring cup, amber polyethylene terephthalate (PET) bottle (118 ml) with polypropylene (PP) screw-cap, and desiccant

The other presentation contains:

- In the pouch: amber polyethylene terephthalate (PET) bottle (118 ml) with polypropylene (PP) screw-cap, and desiccant

The measuring cup has a graduation of 10 ml (max 60 ml) and the oral syringe has a graduation of 0.1 ml and 0.5 ml (max 5 ml).
