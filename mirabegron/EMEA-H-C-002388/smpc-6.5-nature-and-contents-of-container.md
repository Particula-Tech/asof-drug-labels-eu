# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Alu-Alu blisters in cartons containing 10, 20, 30, 50, 60, 90, 100 or 200 tablets.
HDPE bottles with child-resistant closure of polypropylene (PP) and a silica gel desiccant containing 90 tablets.
Each carton contains one bottle.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.
