# 4.6 Fertility, pregnancy and lactation

Women of childbearing potential

Betmiga is not recommended in women of childbearing potential not using contraception.

Pregnancy

There are no or limited amount of data from the use of mirabegron in pregnant women.
Studies in animals have shown reproductive toxicity (see section 5.3).
Betmiga is not recommended during pregnancy.

Breast-feeding

Mirabegron is excreted in the milk of rodents and therefore is predicted to be present in human milk (see section 5.3).
No studies have been conducted to assess the impact of mirabegron on milk production in humans, its presence in human breast milk, or its effects on the breast-fed child.

Betmiga should not be used during breast-feeding.

Fertility

There were no treatment-related effects of mirabegron on fertility in animals (see section 5.3).
The effect of mirabegron on human fertility has not been established.

[Betmiga 8 mg/ml granules for prolonged-release oral suspension]

Betmiga granules for prolonged-release oral suspension is not recommended in women of childbearing potential not using contraception.

Betmiga granules for prolonged-release oral suspension is not recommended during pregnancy.

Betmiga granules for prolonged-release oral suspension should not be used during breast-feeding.
