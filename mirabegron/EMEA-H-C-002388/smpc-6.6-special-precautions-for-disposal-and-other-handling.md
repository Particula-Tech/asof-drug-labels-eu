# 6.6 Special precautions for disposal

Any unused medicinal product or waste material should be disposed of in accordance with local requirements.

[Betmiga 8 mg/ml granules for prolonged-release oral suspension]

Store the oral syringe and adaptor under clean and dry conditions and protect from sunlight and heat.

Use the measuring cup (if provided) to reconstitute the granules prior to administration (see below for instructions on reconstitution).
After reconstitution with 100 ml of water, the oral suspension is pale brownish yellow suspension.

After the granules have been reconstituted, shake the bottle vigorously for 1 minute before each use.
The oral syringe provided with Betmiga granules for prolonged-release oral suspension must be used with the adaptor to measure and administer the correct dose.
Instructions for reconstitution

1.
Open the carton and remove the pouch, syringe, and adaptor.
Keep the bottle in the unopened pouch until the time of reconstitution.
2.
When ready to perform the reconstitution, open the pouch and remove the bottle and measuring cup.
3.
Throw away the empty pouch and desiccant.
Do not swallow the desiccant.
4.
Tap the top of the closed bottle several times to loosen the granules.
5.
Place the bottle on a flat surface and remove the cap.
6.
Measure a total of 100 ml of water and pour it into the bottle.
If a measuring cup is provided, use the measuring cup to first measure 50 ml of water and pour it into the bottle.
Use the cup again to measure another 50 ml of water and pour it into the bottle.
7.
Replace the cap on the bottle.
Immediately shake vigorously for 1 minute, then let it stand for 10 to 30 minutes.
Again, shake vigorously for 1 minute.
8.
Record 28 days from the date of reconstitution on the bottle label.
9.
10.
Firmly press the adaptor into the neck of the bottle.
11.
Make sure that the top of the adaptor is even with the top of the bottleneck.
The adaptor should remain in the neck of the bottle until the end of the 28-day shelf life period.
12.

Cleaning of the oral syringe

After use, clean the oral syringe with warm water.
The oral syringe can be used throughout the 28-day shelf life after first opening (see section 6.3).

Disposal

Throw away any remaining medicinal product 28 days after reconstitution.
