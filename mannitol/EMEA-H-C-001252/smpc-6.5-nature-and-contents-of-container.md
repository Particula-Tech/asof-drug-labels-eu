# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Bronchitol 40 mg capsules are presented in double aluminium blisters in cartons containing 10 or 280 capsules for initial dose and treatment use respectively.

The initiation dose carton contains 1 blister strip (of 10 capsules) and one inhaler device.

The 2-week carton contains 28 blister strips (of 10 capsules each) and two inhaler devices.
