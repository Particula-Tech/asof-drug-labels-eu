# 6.3 Shelf life

3 years.

[Nucala 100 mg powder for solution for injection]

4 years.

After reconstitution

Chemical and physical stability of the reconstituted medicinal product have been demonstrated for 8 hours when stored below 30°C.

From a microbiological point of view, unless the method of reconstitution precludes the risk of microbial contamination, the product must be used immediately.
If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions are the responsibility of user.
