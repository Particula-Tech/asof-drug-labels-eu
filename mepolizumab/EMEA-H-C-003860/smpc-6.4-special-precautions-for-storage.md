# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store in a refrigerator (2°C - 8°C).
Do not freeze.
Store in the original carton in order to protect from light.

If necessary, the Nucala pre-filled pen and pre-filled syringe can be removed from the refrigerator and kept in the unopened pack for up to 7 days at room temperature (up to 30°C), when protected from light.
The pack should be discarded if left out of the refrigerator for more than 7 days.
The pre-filled pen or pre-filled syringe must be administered within 8 hours once the pack is opened.
The pack should be discarded if not administered within 8 hours.

[Nucala 100 mg powder for solution for injection]

Store below 25°C.
Keep the vial in the outer carton in order to protect from light.

For storage conditions after reconstitution of the medicinal product, see section 6.3.
