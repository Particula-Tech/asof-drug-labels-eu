# 4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

Insulin requirements may be increased by medicinal products with hyperglycaemic activity, such as oral contraceptives, corticosteroids, or thyroid replacement therapy, danazol, beta2 stimulants (such as ritodrine, salbutamol, terbutaline).
Insulin requirements may be reduced in the presence of medicinal products with hypoglycaemic activity, such as oral hypoglycaemics, salicylates (for example, acetylsalicylic acid), sulpha antibiotics, certain antidepressants, certain angiotensin converting enzyme inhibitors ( captopril, enalapril), beta blockers, octreotide or alcohol.

Humalog should not be mixed with animal insulins.

The physician should be consulted when using other medications in addition to Humalog.
