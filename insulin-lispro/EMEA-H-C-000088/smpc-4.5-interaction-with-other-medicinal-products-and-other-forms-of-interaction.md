# 4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

Insulin requirements may be increased by drugs with hyperglycaemic activity, such as oral contraceptives, corticosteroids, or thyroid replacement therapy, danazol, beta 2 stimulants (ritodrine, salbutamol, terbutaline).

Insulin requirements may be reduced in the presence of drugs with hypoglycemic activity, such as oral hypoglycemics, salicylates (for example, aspirin), sulfa antibiotics, and certain antidepressants, certain angiotensin converting enzyme inhibitors ( captopril, enalapril), beta blockers, octreotide, alcohol.

Humalog should not be mixed with animal insulins.

The physician should be consulted when using other medications in addition to Humalog.
