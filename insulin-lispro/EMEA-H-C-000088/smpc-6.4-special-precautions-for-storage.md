# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Do not freeze.
Do not expose to excessive heat or direct sunlight.

Unopened vials
Store in a refrigerator (2°C - 8°C).

After first use
Store in a refrigerator (2°C - 8°C) or below 30°C.

[Humalog 100 U/ml, solution for injection in cartridge]

Unused cartridge

After cartridge insertion
Store below 30°C.
Do not refrigerate.
The pen with the inserted cartridge should not be stored with the needle attached.
