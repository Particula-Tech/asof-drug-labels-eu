# 6.5 Nature and content of container

The solution is filled aseptically into Type I flint glass cartridges.
The glass conforms to Ph Eur requirements.
The containers are then sealed with butyl or halobutyl disk seals and plunger heads.
Dimethicone or silicone emulsion may be used to treat the cartridge plunger, and/or the glass cartridge.
The closures are secured with aluminum seals.
