# 6.1 List of excipients

Each vial contains insulin lispro and the following excipients;
(a) m-Cresol distilled [3.15 mg/ml] (b) glycerol (c) dibasic sodium phosphate.
7H2O (d) zinc oxide (e) water for injection (f) hydrochloric acid and (g) sodium hydroxide.
These are included as;
(a) preservative and stabiliser (b) tonicity modifier (c) a buffering agent (d) stabiliser (e) a vehicle (f) and (g) pH adjustment.
