# 6.1 List of Excipients

Each vial will contain insulin lispro and the following excipients;
(a) m-Cresol distilled [3.15mg/mL] (b) glycerol (c) dibasic sodium phosphate.7H2O (d) zinc oxide (e) water for injection (f) hydrochloric acid and (g) sodium hydroxide.
These are included as;
(a) preservative and stabilizer (b) tonicity modifier (c) a buffering agent (d) stabilizer (e) a vehicle (f) pH adjustment and (g) pH adjustment respectively.
