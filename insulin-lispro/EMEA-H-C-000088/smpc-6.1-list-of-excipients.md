# 6.1 List of excipients

Each vial contains insulin lispro and the following excipients:
Protamine sulphate, m-Cresol [2.20 mg/ml], phenol [1.00 mg/ml], glycerol, dibasic sodium phosphate.7H2O, zinc oxide, water for injections.
Hydrochloric acid and sodium hydroxide may be used to adjust pH to 7.0-7.8.
