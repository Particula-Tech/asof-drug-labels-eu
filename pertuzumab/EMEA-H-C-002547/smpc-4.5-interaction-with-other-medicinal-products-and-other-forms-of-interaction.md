# 4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

No pharmacokinetic (PK) interactions were observed between Perjeta and trastuzumab, or between Perjeta and docetaxel in a sub-study of 37 patients in the randomised, pivotal trial CLEOPATRA.
In addition, in the population PK analysis, no evidence of a drug-drug interaction has been shown between Perjeta and trastuzumab and between Perjeta and docetaxel.

Four studies have evaluated the effects of Perjeta on the PK of co-administered cytotoxic agents, docetaxel, gemcitabine, erlotinib and capecitabine, respectively.
There was no evidence of any PK interaction between Perjeta and any of these agents.
The PK of Perjeta in these studies was comparable to those observed in single-agent studies.
