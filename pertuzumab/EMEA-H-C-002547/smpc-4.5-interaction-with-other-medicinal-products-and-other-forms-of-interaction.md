# 4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

No pharmacokinetic (PK) interactions were observed between pertuzumab and trastuzumab, or between pertuzumab and docetaxel in a sub-study of 37 patients in the randomised, pivotal trial CLEOPATRA in metastatic breast cancer.
In addition, in the population PK analysis, no evidence of a drug-drug interaction has been shown between pertuzumab and trastuzumab or between pertuzumab and docetaxel.
This absence of drug-drug interaction was confirmed by pharmacokinetic data from the NEOSPHERE trial in the neoadjuvant setting.

Four studies have evaluated the effects of pertuzumab on the PK of co-administered cytotoxic agents, docetaxel, gemcitabine, erlotinib and capecitabine.
There was no evidence of any PK interaction between pertuzumab and any of these agents.
The PK of pertuzumab in these studies was comparable to those observed in single-agent studies.
