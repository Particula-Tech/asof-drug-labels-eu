# 4.9 Overdose

In a study of healthy volunteers, symptoms of flushing, dizziness and increased heart rate were assessed as intolerable at regadenoson doses greater than 0.02 mg/kg.

Treatment

Aminophylline may be used to attenuate severe or persistent adverse reactions to regadenoson.
Administration of aminophylline, solely for the purpose of terminating a regadenoson-induced seizure is not recommended (see section 4.4).
