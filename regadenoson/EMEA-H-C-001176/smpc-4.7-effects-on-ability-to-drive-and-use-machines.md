# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

No studies on the effects of Rapiscan on the ability to drive and use machines have been performed.
Rapiscan administration may result in adverse reactions such as dizziness, headache, and dyspnoea (see section 4.8) soon after administration.
However, most adverse reactions are mild and transient, resolving within 30 minutes after receiving Rapiscan.
Therefore, Rapiscan would be expected to have no or negligible influence on the ability to drive or operate machinery once treatment has been completed and these reactions have resolved.
The physician is advised to provide a recommendation for the individual patient.
