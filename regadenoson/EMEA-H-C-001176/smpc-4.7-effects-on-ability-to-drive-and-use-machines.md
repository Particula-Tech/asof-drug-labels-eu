# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

Regadenoson administration may result in adverse reactions such as dizziness, headache, and dyspnoea (see section 4.8) soon after administration.
However, most adverse reactions are mild and transient, resolving within 30 minutes after receiving regadenoson.
Therefore, regadenoson would be expected to have no or negligible influence on the ability to drive or use machines once treatment has been completed and these reactions have resolved.
