# 5.3 Preclinical safety data

Non-clinical data reveal no special hazard for humans based on conventional studies of safety pharmacology, single and repeated dose toxicity, genotoxicity, or embryo-fetal development.
Signs of maternal and fetal toxicity were seen in rats and rabbits (reduced fetal weights, delays in ossification [rats], reduced litter size and number of live fetuses [rabbits]), but not teratogenicity.
Fetal toxicity was noted following repeated daily administration of regadenoson, but at doses sufficiently in excess of the recommended human dose.
Fertility and pre- and post-natal studies have not been conducted.
