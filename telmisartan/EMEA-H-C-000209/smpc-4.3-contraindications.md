# 4.3 Contraindications

� Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients (see 6.1 List of excipients)
� Second and third trimesters of pregnancy and lactation (see 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation)
� Biliary obstructive disorders
� Severe hepatic impairment
� Severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance < 30 ml/min)
