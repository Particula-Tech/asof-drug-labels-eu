# 4.6 Use during pregnancy and lactation

Use in pregnancy (See 4.3 Contra-indications)
Telmisartan is contraindicated during pregnancy.
A switch to a suitable alternative treatment should be carried out in advance of a planned pregnancy.

If pregnancy is diagnosed telmisartan should be discontinued as soon as possible.
In the second and third trimesters, substances that act directly on the renin-angiotensin-system can cause injury and even death in the developing foetus.

Use in lactation (See section 4.3 Contra-indications)
Telmisartan is contraindicated during lactation since it is not known whether it is excreted in human milk.
