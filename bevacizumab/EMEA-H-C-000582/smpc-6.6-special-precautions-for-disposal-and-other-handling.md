# 6.6 Special precautions for disposal and other handling

Avastin should be prepared by a healthcare professional using aseptic technique to ensure the sterility of the prepared solution.

The necessary amount of bevacizumab should be withdrawn and diluted to the required administration volume with sodium chloride 9 mg/ml (0.9%) solution for injection.
The concentration of the final bevacizumab solution should be kept within the range of 1.4-16.5 mg/ml.

Parenteral medicinal products should be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.

Avastin is for single-use only, as the product contains no preservatives.
Any unused product or waste material should be disposed of in accordance with local requirements.

No incompatibilities between Avastin and polyvinyl chloride or polyolefine bags or infusion sets have been observed.
