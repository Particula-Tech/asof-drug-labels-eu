# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store in a refrigerator (2 °C to 8 °C).
Do not freeze.
Store in the original carton in order to protect from light.

A single Benlysta pre-filled syringe or pre-filled pen can be stored at temperatures up to a maximum of 25 °C for a period of up to 12 hours.
The syringe or pen must be protected from light, and discarded if not used within the 12 hour period.

[Benlysta 120 mg powder for concentrate for solution for infusion.]

For storage conditions after reconstitution and dilution of the medicinal product, see section 6.3.
