# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

3 mL, low density polyethylene ampoule.

Quinsair is supplied as a 28-day pack.
This comprises an inner carton box containing 56 (14 sachets of 4) ampoules.
The outer carton box also contains one Zirela Nebuliser Handset packaged in its own carton box with the Manufacturer's Instruction for Use.
