# 4.9 Overdose

In the event of overdose, symptomatic treatment should be implemented.
The patient should be observed and appropriate hydration maintained.
ECG monitoring should be undertaken because of the possibility of QT interval prolongation.
Haemodialysis, including peritoneal dialysis and continuous ambulatory peritoneal dialysis (CAPD), are not effective in removing levofloxacin from the body.
No specific antidote exists.
