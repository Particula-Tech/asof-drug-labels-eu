# 4.9 Overdose

Experience with overdose of SPRYCEL in clinical studies is limited to isolated cases.
The highest reported dosage ingested was 280 mg per day for one week with no associated clinical symptoms.
Since SPRYCEL is associated with severe myelosuppression (see section 4.4), patients who ingested more than the recommended dosage should be closely monitored for myelosuppression and appropriate supportive treatment given.
