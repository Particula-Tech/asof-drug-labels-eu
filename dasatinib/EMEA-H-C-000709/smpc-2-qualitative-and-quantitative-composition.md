[SPRYCEL 10 mg/mL powder for oral suspension]

One bottle of powder for oral suspension contains 990 mg of dasatinib (as monohydrate).
After constitution, one bottle contains 99 mL of oral suspension.
Each mL of oral suspension contains 10 mg of dasatinib (as monohydrate).

Each mL of oral suspension contains approximately 291 mg of sucrose, 2.1 mg of sodium, 0.25 mg of sodium benzoate, 0.25 mg of benzoic acid, 0.017 mg of benzyl alcohol and <10 ppm of sulphur dioxide (E220).
