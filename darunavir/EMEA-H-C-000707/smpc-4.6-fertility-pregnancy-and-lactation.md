# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

Pregnancy
There are no adequate and well controlled studies with darunavir in pregnant women.
Studies in animals do not indicate direct harmful effects with respect to pregnancy, embryonal/foetal development, parturition or postnatal development (see section 5.3).
PREZISTA co-administered with 100 mg ritonavir should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk.

Lactation
It is not known whether darunavir is excreted in human milk.
Studies in rats have demonstrated that darunavir is excreted in milk and at high levels (1,000 mg/kg/day) resulted in toxicity.
Because of both the potential for HIV transmission and the potential for adverse events in nursing infants, mothers should be instructed not to breast-feed under any circumstances if they are receiving PREZISTA.

Fertility
No human data on the effect of darunavir on fertility are available.
There was no effect on mating or fertility with darunavir treatment in rats (see section 5.3).
