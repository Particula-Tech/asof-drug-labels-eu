# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

No studies on the effects of PREZISTA in combination with ritonavir on the ability to drive and use machines have been performed.
However, dizziness has been reported in some patients during treatment with regimens containing PREZISTA co-administered with low dose ritonavir and should be borne in mind when considering a patient’s ability to drive or operate machinery (see section 4.8).
