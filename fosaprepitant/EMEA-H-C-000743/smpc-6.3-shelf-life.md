# 6.3 Shelf-life

2 years.

After reconstitution and dilution, chemical and physical in-use stability has been demonstrated for 24 hours at 25°C.

From a microbiological point of view, the medicinal product should be used immediately.
If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and would normally not be longer than 24 hours at 2 to 8°C.
