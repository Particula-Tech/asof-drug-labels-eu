# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

No studies on the effects of IVEMEND on the ability to drive and use machines have been performed.
However, when driving vehicles or operating machines, it should be taken into account that dizziness and fatigue have been reported after using IVEMEND (see section 4.8).
