# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

10 ml Type I clear glass vial with a chlorobutyl or bromobutyl rubber stopper and an aluminum seal with a blue plastic flip off cap.

IVEMEND will be available as a pack of 1 vial of 115 mg fosaprepitant or 10 vials of 115 mg fosaprepitant.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.
