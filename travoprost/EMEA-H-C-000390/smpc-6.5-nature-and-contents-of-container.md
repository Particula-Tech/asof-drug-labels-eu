# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

4 mL oval Polypropylene (PP) or Low Density Polyethylene (LDPE) bottle and PP or LDPE dispensing plug with a PP screw cap, presented in an overwrap.
Each 4 mL bottle will contain 2.5 mL of solution.

Cartons containing 1 or 3 bottles.
Not all pack sizes may be marketed.
