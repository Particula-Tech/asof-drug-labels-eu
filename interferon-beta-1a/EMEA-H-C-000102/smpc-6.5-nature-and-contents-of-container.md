# 6.5 Nature and content of the container

AVONEX (Interferon beta-1a) is available as a package of four individual doses of: AVONEX in a 3 mL clear glass vial with a 13 mm bromobutyl rubber stopper and aluminum seal.
It is provided with a 1 mL pre-filled glass syringe of solvent for reconstitution and 2 needles.
