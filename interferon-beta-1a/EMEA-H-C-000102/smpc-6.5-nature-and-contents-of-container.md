# 6.5 Nature and content of container

AVONEX (Interferon beta-1a) is available as a package of four individual doses of: AVONEX in a 3 ml clear glass vial with a 13 mm bromobutyl rubber stopper and aluminium seal.
It is provided with a 1 ml pre-filled glass syringe of solvent for reconstitution and 2 needles.
