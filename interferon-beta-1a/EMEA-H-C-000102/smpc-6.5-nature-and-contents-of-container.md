# 6.5 Nature and content of container

AVONEX (Interferon beta-1a) is available as a package of four individual doses of AVONEX

in a

3 ml clear glass vial with BIO-SET device and a 13 mm bromobutyl rubber stopper.
It is provided with a 1 ml pre-filled glass syringe of solvent for reconstitution and 1 needle.
