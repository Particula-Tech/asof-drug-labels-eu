# 4.3 Contraindications

AVONEX (Interferon beta-1a) is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to natural or recombinant interferon beta, human serum albumin or any other component of the formulation.

AVONEX is contraindicated in pregnant patients (also see 4.6 Use during pregnancy and lactation), patients with severe depressive disorders and/or suicidal ideation and in epileptic patients with a history of seizures not adequately controlled by treatment.
