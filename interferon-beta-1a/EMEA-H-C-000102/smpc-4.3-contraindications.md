# 4.3 Contraindications

AVONEX is contraindicated in patients with hypersensitivity to natural or recombinant interferon beta, or to any of the excipients.

AVONEX is contraindicated in pregnant patients (also see 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation).

AVONEX is contraindicated in patients with severe depressive disorders and/or suicidal ideation.

AVONEX is contraindicated in epileptic patients with a history of seizures not adequately controlled by treatment.
