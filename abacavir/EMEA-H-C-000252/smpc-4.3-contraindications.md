# 4.3 Contraindications

Ziagen is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to abacavir or to any of the excipients of Ziagen tablets.

Ziagen is contraindicated in patients with severe hepatic impairment.

[Ziagen 20 mg/ml oral solution]

Ziagen is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to abacavir or to any of the excipients of Ziagen oral solution.
