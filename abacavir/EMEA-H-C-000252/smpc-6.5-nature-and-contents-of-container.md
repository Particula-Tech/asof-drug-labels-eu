# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Ziagen Tablets are available in polyvinyl chloride/foil blister packs containing 60 tablets.

[Ziagen 20 mg/ml oral solution]

Ziagen Oral Solution is supplied in high density polyethylene bottles with child-resistant closures, containing 240 ml of oral solution.
A 10 ml polypropylene oral dosing syringe and a polyethylene adapter are also included in the pack.
