# 4.2 Posology and method of administration

Ziagen should be prescribed by physicians experienced in the management of HIV infection.

The recommended dose of Ziagen in adults (18 years and over) is 300 mg (one tablet) twice daily.

Ziagen can be taken with or without food.

Ziagen is available as an oral solution for use in those patients for whom the tablets are inappropriate.

Renal impairment: No dosage adjustment of Ziagen is necessary in patients with renal dysfunction.
However, Ziagen should be avoided in patients with end-stage renal disease (see 5.2 Pharmacokinetic properties).

Hepatic impairment: Abacavir is primarily metabolised by the liver.
No dosage adjustment of Ziagen is required in patients with mild hepatic impairment.
Data to support dosing recommendations in patients with moderate hepatic impairment are not currently available, therefore administration of Ziagen should be avoided in these patients.
Ziagen is contraindicated in patients with severe hepatic impairment.

Elderly: No pharmacokinetic data is currently available in patients over 65 years of age.

[Ziagen 20 mg/ml oral solution]

The recommended dose of Ziagen in adults (18 years and over) is 15 ml (300 mg) twice daily.

Ziagen is also available as a tablet formulation.
