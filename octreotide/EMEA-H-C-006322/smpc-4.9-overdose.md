# 4.9 Overdose

A limited number of accidental overdoses of octreotide injections in adults have been reported.
The doses ranged from 2 400-6 000 micrograms/day administered by continuous infusion (100-250 micrograms/hour) or subcutaneously (1 000 micrograms three times a day).
The adverse events reported were arrhythmia, hypotension, cardiac arrest, brain hypoxia, pancreatitis, hepatic steatosis, diarrhoea, weakness, lethargy, weight loss, hepatomegaly, and lactic acidosis.

No unexpected adverse events have been reported in cancer patients receiving subcutaneous octreotide at doses of 3 000-30 000 micrograms/day in divided doses.

The management of overdose is symptomatic.
