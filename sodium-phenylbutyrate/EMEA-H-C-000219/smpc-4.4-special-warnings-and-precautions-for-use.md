# 4.4 Special warnings and special precautions for use

Each AMMONAPS tablet contains 62 mg or 2.7 mmol of sodium, corresponding to 2.5 g or 108 mmol of sodium per 20 g of sodium phenylbutyrate, the maximum daily dose. .
It should therefore be used with caution in patients with congestive heart failure or severe renal insufficiency, and in clinical states where there is sodium retention with oedema.

Since metabolism and excretion of sodium phenylbutyrate involve the liver and kidneys, AMMONAPS should be used with caution in patients with hepatic or renal insufficiency.
