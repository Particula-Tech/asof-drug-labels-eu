# 4.9 Overdose

There is no direct experience of human overdose and no specific antidote.
However, early clinical study data in published literature at doses greater than 20 times higher than the current therapeutic dose, reported increased myelosuppression including prolonged neutropenia and thrombocytopenia.
Toxicity is likely to manifest as exacerbations of adverse drug reactions, primarily myelosuppression.
Treatment for overdose should be supportive.
