# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened vial
3 years.

Reconstituted and diluted solution
Within 15 minutes of reconstitution, the concentrate (in 10 ml of sterile water for injections) must be further diluted with cold (2°C - 8°C) infusion fluids.
This prepared diluted solution for intravenous infusion can be stored at 2°C - 8°C for up to a maximum of 3 hours, followed by up to 1 hour at room temperature (20°C - 25°C) before administration.

From a microbiological point of view, the product should be used within the time period recommended above.
It is the responsibility of the user to follow the recommended storage times and conditions and ensure that reconstitution has taken place in aseptic conditions.
