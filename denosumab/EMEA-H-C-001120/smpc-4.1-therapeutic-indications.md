# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

Treatment of osteoporosis in postmenopausal women at increased risk of fractures.
Prolia significantly reduces the risk of vertebral, non vertebral and hip fractures.

Treatment of bone loss associated with hormone ablation in men with prostate cancer at increased risk of fractures (see section 5.1).
In men with prostate cancer receiving hormone ablation, Prolia significantly reduces the risk of vertebral fractures.
