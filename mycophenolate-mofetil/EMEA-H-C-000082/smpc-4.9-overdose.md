# 4.9 Overdose

The experience with overdose of CellCept in humans is very limited.
The events received from reports of overdose fall within the known safety profile of the drug.

Haemodialysis would not be expected to remove clinically significant amounts of MPA or MPAG.
By interfering with enterohepatic circulation of the drug, bile acid sequestrants, such as cholestyramine, reduce the MPA AUC.
