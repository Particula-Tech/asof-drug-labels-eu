# 4.9 Overdose

There has been no reported experience of overdosage of mycophenolate mofetil in humans.

At clinically encountered concentrations, MPA and MPAG are not removed by haemodialysis.
However, at high MPAG plasma concentrations (> 100 µg/ml), small amounts of MPAG are removed.
By interfering with enterohepatic circulation of the drug, bile acid sequestrants, such as cholestyramine reduce the MPA AUC.
