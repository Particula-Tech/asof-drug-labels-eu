# 4.3 Contra-Indications

Allergic reactions to CellCept have been observed.
Therefore, CellCept is contraindicated in patients with a hypersensitivity to mycophenolate mofetil or mycophenolic acid.
For information on use in pregnancy and contraceptive requirements, see 4.6 Use during pregnancy and lactation.
