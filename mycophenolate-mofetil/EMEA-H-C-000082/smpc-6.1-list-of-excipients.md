# 6.1 List of excipients

Excipients of CellCept tablets are microcrystalline cellulose, polyvidone (K-90), croscarmellose sodium and magnesium stearate.
The tablet coating consists of hydroxypropyl methylcellulose, hydroxypropyl cellulose, titanium dioxide (E171), polyethylene glycol 400, indigo carmine aluminium lake (E132), and red iron oxide (E172).
