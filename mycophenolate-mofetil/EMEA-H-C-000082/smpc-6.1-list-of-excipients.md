# 6.1 List of excipients

Excipients of CellCept capsules are pregelatinized maize starch, croscarmellose sodium, polyvidone (K-90) and magnesium stearate.
The capsule shells contain gelatin, indigo carmine (FP, E132), yellow iron oxide (FP, E172), red iron oxide (FP, E172), titanium dioxide (EP, E171), black iron oxide (FP, E172) potassium hydroxide, shellac .
