# 5.2 Pharmacokinetic properties

Single intravenous infusions of 1, 5, 10 or 20 mg/kg of infliximab yielded dose proportional increases in the maximum serum concentration (Cmax) and area under the concentration-time curve (AUC).
The volume of distribution at steady state (Vd) was not dependent on the administered dose and indicated that infliximab is predominantly distributed within the vascular compartment.
No time-dependency of the pharmacokinetics was observed.
The elimination pathways for infliximab have not been characterised.
At the recommended single dose of 5 mg/kg, the median pharmacokinetic parameters derived for infliximab included a Cmax of 118 microgram/ml, Vd of 3.0 litres and a terminal half-life of 9.5 days.
In most patients, infliximab could be detected in serum for 8 weeks at this dose.
No major differences in pharmacokinetics were observed among patients within different demographic groups or with indices of mild impairment of liver or kidney function.

The 3-infusion regimen used to treat fistulising Crohn’s disease (5 mg/kg at 0, 2 and 6 weeks) resulted in a slight accumulation of infliximab in serum.
In most patients, infliximab was detected in serum for 12 weeks (range 4-28 weeks).
