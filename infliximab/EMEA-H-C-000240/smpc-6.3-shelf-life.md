# 6.3 Shelf life

3 years

Chemical and physical in use stability of the reconstituted solution has been demonstrated for 24 hours at room temperature (25�C).
From a microbiological point of view, the product should be used as soon as possible but within 3 hours of reconstitution and dilution.
If not used immediately, in use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and should not be longer than 24 hours at 2 to 8�C.
