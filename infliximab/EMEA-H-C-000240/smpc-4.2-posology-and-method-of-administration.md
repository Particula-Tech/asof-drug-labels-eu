# 4.2 Posology and method of administration

Remicade is for intravenous use in adults and has not been studied in children (0-17 years).

Remicade treatment is to be administered under the supervision of a physician experienced in the diagnosis and treatment of inflammatory bowel diseases.

All patients administered Remicade are to be observed for at least 1-2 hours post infusion for side effects.
Emergency equipment must be available.

Severe, active Crohn’s disease:
5 mg/kg given as an intravenous infusion over a 2-hour period.

Fistulising Crohn’s disease:
An initial 5 mg/kg infusion given over a 2-hour period is to be followed with additional 5 mg/kg infusion doses at 2 and 6 weeks after the first infusion.

Retreatment
If signs and symptoms recur, Remicade can be readministered within 14 weeks following the last infusion.
However, long-term efficacy of retreatment has not been established.

Readministration of Remicade with a drug free interval of 2 to 4 years following a previous infusion has been associated with a delayed hypersensitivity reaction in a significant number of patients (see section 4.4, and section 4.8: “Delayed hypersensitivity”).
After a drug free interval of 15 weeks to 2 years, the risk of delayed hypersensitivity following readministration is not known.
Therefore, after a drug free interval of 15 weeks, readministration can not be recommended.

For preparation and administration instructions, see section 6.6.
