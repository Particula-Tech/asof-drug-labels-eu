# 4.3 Contraindications

Remicade is contraindicated in patients with tuberculosis or other severe infections such as sepsis, abscesses, and opportunistic infections (see section 4.4).

Remicade is contraindicated in patients with moderate or severe heart failure (NYHA class III/IV) (see sections 4.4 and 4.8).

Remicade must not be given to patients with a history of hypersensitivity to infliximab (see section 4.8), to other murine proteins, or to any of the excipients.
