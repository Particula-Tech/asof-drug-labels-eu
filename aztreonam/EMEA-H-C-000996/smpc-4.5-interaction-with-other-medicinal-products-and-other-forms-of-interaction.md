# 4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

No interaction studies have been performed.
However, no evidence of any drug interactions with Cayston were identified from clinical studies in which Cayston was taken concomitantly with bronchodilators, dornase alfa, pancreatic enzymes, azithromycin, tobramycin, oral steroids (less than 10 mg daily/20 mg every other day) and inhaled steroids.
