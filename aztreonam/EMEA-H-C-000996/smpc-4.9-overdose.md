# 4.9 Overdose

Adverse reactions specifically associated with overdose of Cayston have not been identified.
Since the plasma concentration of aztreonam following administration of Cayston (75 mg) is approximately 0.6 µg/ml, compared to serum levels of 54 µg/ml following administration of aztreonam for injection (500 mg), no safety issues associated with Cayston overdose are anticipated.
