# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Powder vial: Type I amber glass vial with siliconised grey rubber stopper and aluminium tear off overseal.

Solvent: 1 ml low density polyethylene ampoule.

Each 28-day pack of Cayston contains 84 vials of lyophilised Cayston and 88 solvent ampoules.
The four additional solvent ampoules are provided in case of spillage.
