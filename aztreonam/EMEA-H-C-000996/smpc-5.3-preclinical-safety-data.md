# 5.3 Preclinical safety data

A 104-week rat inhalation toxicology study to assess the carcinogenic potential of ascending doses of Cayston demonstrated no drug-related increase in malignant tumours.

Genotoxicity (Chromosomal aberration and mouse lymphoma mutation assay) studies with aztreonam were negative.

Fertility, teratology, perinatal and postnatal studies were conducted with aztreonam for injection in rats at daily doses up to 750 mg/kg without adverse effects.
The survival rate during the lactation period was slightly reduced in the offspring of rats that received the highest dose.
