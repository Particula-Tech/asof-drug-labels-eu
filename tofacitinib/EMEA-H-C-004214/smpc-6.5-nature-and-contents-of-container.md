# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

XELJANZ 5 mg film-coated tablets

HDPE bottles with silica gel desiccant and child-resistant polypropylene closure containing 60 or 180 film-coated tablets.

Aluminium foil/PVC backed aluminium foil blisters containing 14 film-coated tablets.
Each pack contains 56, 112, or 182 film-coated tablets.

XELJANZ 10 mg film-coated tablets

HDPE bottles with silica gel desiccant and child-resistant polypropylene closure containing 60 or 180 film-coated tablets.

Aluminium foil/PVC backed aluminium foil blisters containing 14 film-coated tablets.
Each pack contains 56, 112, or 182 film-coated tablets.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[XELJANZ 11 mg prolonged-release tablets]

HDPE bottles with 2 silica gel desiccants and child-resistant, polypropylene closure containing 30 or 90 prolonged-release tablets.

Aluminium foil/PVC backed aluminium foil blisters containing 7 prolonged-release tablets.
Each pack contains 28 or 91 prolonged-release tablets.

[XELJANZ 1 mg/mL oral solution]

White coloured HDPE 250 mL bottles containing 240 mL of oral solution with a child resistant, polypropylene cap with PP liner sealed by aluminium-foil heat-induction seal and a 5 mL oral dosing syringe with 3.2 mL, 4 mL, and 5 mL graduations.

The container closure system also includes a low-density polyethylene (LDPE) press-in bottle adapter (PIBA).

Pack size: each pack contains one bottle, one press-in bottle adapter, and one oral dosing syringe.
