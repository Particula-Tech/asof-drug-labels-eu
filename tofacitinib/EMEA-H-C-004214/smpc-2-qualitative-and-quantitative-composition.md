# 2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION

XELJANZ 5 mg film-coated tablets

Each 5 mg film-coated tablet contains tofacitinib citrate, equivalent to 5 mg tofacitinib.

Excipient with known effect
Each film-coated tablet contains 59.44 mg of lactose.

XELJANZ 10 mg film-coated tablets

Each 10 mg film-coated tablet contains tofacitinib citrate, equivalent to 10 mg tofacitinib.

Excipient with known effect
Each film-coated tablet contains 118.88 mg of lactose.

For the full list of excipients, see section 6.1.
