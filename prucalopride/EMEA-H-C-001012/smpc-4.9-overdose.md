# 4.9 Overdose

In a study in healthy volunteers treatment with prucalopride was well tolerated when given in an up-titrating scheme up to 20 mg once daily (10 times the recommended therapeutic dose).
An overdose may result in symptoms resulting from an exaggeration of the medicinal product’s known pharmacodynamic effects and include headache, nausea and diarrhoea.
Specific treatment is not available for Resolor overdose.
Should an overdose occur, the patient should be treated symptomatically and supportive measures instituted, as required.
Extensive fluid loss by diarrhoea or vomiting may require correction of electrolyte disturbances.
