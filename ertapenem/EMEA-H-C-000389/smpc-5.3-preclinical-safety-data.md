# 5.3 Preclinical safety data

Non-clinical data reveal no special hazard for humans based on conventional studies of safety, pharmacology, repeated-dose toxicity, genotoxicity and toxicity in reproduction.
Decreased neutrophil counts, however, occurred in rats that received high doses of ertapenem, which was not considered a significant safety issue.
Long-term studies in animals to evaluate the carcinogenic potential of ertapenem have not been performed.
