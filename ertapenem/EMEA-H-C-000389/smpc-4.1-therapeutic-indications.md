# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

Treatment of the following infections when caused by bacteria known or very likely to be susceptible to ertapenem and when parenteral therapy is required (see section 4.4 and section 5.1):

- Intra-abdominal infections
- Community acquired pneumonia
- Acute gynaecological infections
- Diabetic foot infections of the skin and soft tissue (see section 4.4)

Consideration should be given to official guidance on the appropriate use of antibacterial agents.
