[Briviact 10 mg/ml oral solution]

300 ml amber glass bottle (type III) with a white child resistant closure (polypropylene) in a box also containing a 5 ml and 10 ml graduated oral dosing syringe (polypropylene, polyethylene) and an adaptor for the syringe (polyethylene).

[Briviact 10 mg/ml solution for injection/infusion]

10 mg/ml solution for injection/infusion is packed in 6 ml nominal capacity glass vials (type I) with siliconized bromobutyl rubber stoppers and sealed with an aluminium/polypropylene tear off cap.
Each single use vial contains an extractable volume of not less than 5.0 ml of solution for injection/infusion.

Each carton contains 10 vials.
