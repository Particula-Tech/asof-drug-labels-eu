# 6.3 Shelf life

4 years.

[Briviact 10 mg/ml oral solution]

After first opening: 5 months

[Briviact 10 mg/ml solution for injection/infusion]

Following dilution, brivaracetam solution for injection/infusion was found to be physically compatible and chemically stable when mixed with the diluents listed in the section 6.6 for 24 hours and stored in PVC or polyolefin bags at temperature up to 25°C.
From a microbiological point of view, the product should be used immediately after dilution.
If not used immediately, in-use storage time and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user.
