# 4.9 Overdose

Symptoms

There is limited clinical experience with brivaracetam overdose in humans.
Somnolence and dizziness have been reported in a healthy subject taking a single dose of 1,400 mg of brivaracetam.

Management of overdose

There is no specific antidote for overdose with brivaracetam.
Treatment of an overdose should include general supportive measures.
Since less than 10 % of brivaracetam is excreted in urine, haemodialysis is not expected to significantly enhance brivaracetam clearance (see section 5.2).
