# 4.9 Overdose

There have been very few cases of overdose.

In the case of overdosage (a suicide attempt) for which the highest memantine dose has been reported, the patient survived the oral intake of up to 400 mg memantine with effects on the central nervous system (that is, restlessness, psychosis, visual hallucinations, proconvulsiveness, somnolence, stupor, and unconsciousness) that resolved without permanent sequelae.

Treatment of overdosage should be symptomatic.
