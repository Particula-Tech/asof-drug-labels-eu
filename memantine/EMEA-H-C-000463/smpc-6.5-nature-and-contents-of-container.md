# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Blister packs containing either 7, 10, 14 or 20 tablets per blister strip.
Pack sizes of 28, 30, 49 x 1, 50, 56, 56 x 1, 98 x 1,100, 100 x 1, 112 or 1000 (20 x 50) tablets are presented.
The pack sizes 49 x 1, 56 x1, 98 x 1 and 100 x 1 film-coated tablets are presented in unit dose blister.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[Ebixa 10 mg/g oral drops, solution.]

Brown glass bottles (Hydrolytic Class III) with dropper containing either 20, 50, 100 g or 10 x 50 g solution.
