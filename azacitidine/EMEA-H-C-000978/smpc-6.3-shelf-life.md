# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened powder vial:
4 years

After reconstitution:
Chemical and physical in-use stability of the reconstituted medicinal product has been demonstrated at 25°C for 45 minutes and at 2°C to 8°C for 8 hours.

From a microbiological point of view, the reconstituted product should be used immediately.
If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and must not be longer than 8 hours at 2°C to 8°C.
