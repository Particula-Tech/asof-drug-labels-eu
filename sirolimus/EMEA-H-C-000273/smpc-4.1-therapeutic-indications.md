# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

Rapamune is indicated for the prophylaxis of organ rejection in adult patients at low to moderate immunological risk receiving a renal transplant.
It is recommended that Rapamune be used initially in combination with cyclosporine microemulsion and corticosteroids for 2 to 3 months.
Rapamune may be continued as maintenance therapy with corticosteroids only if cyclosporine can be progressively discontinued (refer to sections 4.2 and 5.1).
