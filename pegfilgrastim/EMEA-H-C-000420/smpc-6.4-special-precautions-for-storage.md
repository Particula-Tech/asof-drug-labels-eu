# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store in a refrigerator (2°C – 8°C).

Neulasta may be exposed to room temperature (not above 30°C) for a maximum single period of up to 72 hours.
Neulasta left at room temperature for more than 72 hours should be discarded.

The pre-filled syringe for use with the on-body injector may be exposed at room temperature for no longer than 36 hours prior to filling the on-body injector.

Do not freeze.
Accidental exposure to freezing temperatures for a single period of less than 24 hours does not adversely affect the stability of Neulasta.

Keep the container in the outer carton in order to protect from light.
