# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

0.6 ml of solution for injection in a pre-filled syringe (Type I glass), with a rubber stopper, and with a stainless steel needle.
Pack size of one, in either blistered, with or without an automatic needle guard or non-blistered packaging.
Single use only.

The needle cover of the pre-filled syringe contains dry natural rubber (a derivative of latex) (see section 4.4).

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.
