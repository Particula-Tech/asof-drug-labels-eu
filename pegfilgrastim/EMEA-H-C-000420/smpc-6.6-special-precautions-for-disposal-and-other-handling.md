# 6.6 Special precautions for disposal and other handling

Before administration, Neulasta solution should be inspected visually for particulate matter.
Only a solution that is clear and colourless should be injected.

Excessive shaking may aggregate pegfilgrastim, rendering it biologically inactive.

Allow the pre-filled syringe to reach room temperature before injecting.
Any unused product or waste material should be disposed of in accordance with local requirements.
