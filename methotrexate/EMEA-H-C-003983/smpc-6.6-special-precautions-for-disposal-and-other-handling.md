# 6.6 Special precautions for disposal and other handling

Handling and disposal must be consistent with that of other cytotoxic preparations in accordance with local requirements.
Pregnant health care personnel should not handle and/or administer methotrexate.

Methotrexate should not come into contact with the skin or mucosa.
In the event of contamination, the affected area must be rinsed immediately with ample amount of water.
Nordimet is for single use only and any unused solution must be discarded.

Any unused product or waste material should be disposed of in accordance with local requirements for cytotoxic agents.
