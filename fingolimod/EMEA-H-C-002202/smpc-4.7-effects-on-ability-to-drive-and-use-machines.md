# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

Gilenya has no or negligible influence on the ability to drive and use machines.
However, dizziness or drowsiness may occasionally occur when initiating therapy with Gilenya.
On initiation of Gilenya treatment it is recommended that patients be observed for a period of 6 hours (see section 4.4, Bradyarrhythmia).
