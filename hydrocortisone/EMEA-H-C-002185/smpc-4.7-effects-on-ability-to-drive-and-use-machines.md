# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

Plenadren has minor influence on the ability to drive and use machines.
Fatigue and episodes of short- -lasting vertigo have been reported.

Untreated and poorly replaced adrenal insufficiency may affect the ability to drive and use machines.
