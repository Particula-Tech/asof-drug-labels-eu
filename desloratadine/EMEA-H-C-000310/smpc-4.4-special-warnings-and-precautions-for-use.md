# 4.4 Special warnings and special precautions for use

Efficacy and safety of Azomyr tablets in children under 12 years of age have not been established.

In the case of severe renal insufficiency, Azomyr should be used with caution (see 5.2).

[Azomyr 0.5 mg/ml syrup]

Efficacy and safety of Azomyr syrup in children under 2 years of age have not been established.

This medicinal product contains sucrose and sorbitol; thus, patients with rare hereditary problems of fructose intolerance, glucose-galactose malabsorption or sucrase-isomaltase insufficiency should not take this medicine.

[Azomyr 5 mg oral lyophilisate]

In the case of severe renal insufficiency, Azomyr should be used with caution.

This product contains aspartame 1.75 mg per dose.
Aspartame is a source of phenylalanine, which may be harmful for people with phenylketonuria.
