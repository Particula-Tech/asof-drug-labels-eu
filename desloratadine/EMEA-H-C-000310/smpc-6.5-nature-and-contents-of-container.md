# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Azomyr syrup is supplied in bottles of 30, 50, 60, 100, 120, 150, 225, and 300 ml in type III amber glass bottles closed with a childproof polypropylene cap.
The caps have a liner made of Low Density Polyethylene (LDPE), polyethylene foam, ethylenevinylacetate (EVA), and polyvinylidene chloride (PvDC).
LDPE is the product contact surface.
Supplied with a rigid, transparent, polystyrene measuring spoon, calibrated at 2.5 ml and 5 ml.
Not all pack sizes may be marketed.
