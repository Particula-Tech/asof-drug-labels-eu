# 4.8 Undesirable effects

At the recommended dose of 5 mg daily, undesirable effects with Azomyr were reported in 4 % of patients in excess of those treated with placebo.

The frequency of adverse events in excess of placebo is:

> 1/100, ≤ 1/10 headache
> 1/1,000, ≤ 1/100 dry mouth
> 1/1,000, ≤ 1/100 fatigue
