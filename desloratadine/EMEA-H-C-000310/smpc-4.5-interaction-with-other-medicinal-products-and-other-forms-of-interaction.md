# 4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

No clinically relevant interactions were observed in clinical trials with Azomyr tablets in which erythromycin or ketoconazole were co-administered (see 5.1).
However, the enzyme responsible for the metabolism of desloratadine has not been identified yet, and therefore, some interactions with other drugs can not be fully excluded.

In a clinical pharmacology trial Azomyr taken concomitantly with alcohol did not potentiate the performance impairing effects of alcohol (see 5.1).
