# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

No teratogenic or mutagenic effects were observed in animal trials with desloratadine (see 5.3).
Since no clinical data on exposed pregnancies are available with desloratadine, the safe use of Azomyr during pregnancy has not been established.
Azomyr is not to be used during pregnancy unless the potential benefits outweigh the risks.

Desloratadine is excreted into breast milk, therefore the use of Azomyr is not recommended in breast-feeding women.
