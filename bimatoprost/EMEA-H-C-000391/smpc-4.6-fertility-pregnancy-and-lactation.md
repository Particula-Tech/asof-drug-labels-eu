# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

Pregnancy
There are no adequate data from the use of bimatoprost in pregnant women.
Animal studies have shown reproductive toxicity at high maternotoxic doses (see section 5.3).

LUMIGAN should not be used during pregnancy unless clearly necessary.
Lactation
It is unknown whether bimatoprost is excreted in human breast milk.
Animal studies have shown excretion of bimatoprost in milk.
A risk to the suckling child cannot be excluded.
A decision must be made whether to discontinue breast-feeding or to continue/abstain from LUMIGAN therapy, taking into account the benefit of breast-feeding for the child and the benefit of therapy for the woman.
