# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

Pregnancy

There are no adequate data from the use of bimatoprost in pregnant women.
Animal studies have shown reproductive toxicity at high maternotoxic doses (see section 5.3).

LUMIGAN should not be used during pregnancy unless clearly necessary.

Lactation
It is not known if bimatoprost is excreted in human milk, however, this substance is excreted in rat milk after intravenous administration.
It is recommended that LUMIGAN is not used in nursing mothers.
