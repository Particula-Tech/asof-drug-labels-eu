# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

TAGRISSO as monotherapy is indicated for:

- the first-line treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic non-small cell lung

cancer (NSCLC) with activating epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) mutations.

- the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic EGFR T790M mutation-positive

NSCLC.
