# 4.9 Overdose

There is no known antidote to Ketoconazole HRA.
The maximal dose that was used for treatment of Cushing’s syndrome is 1600 mg/day.
In the event of accidental overdose, treatment consists of supportive measures.
Within the first hour after ingestion gastric lavage may be performed.
Activated charcoal may be given if considered appropriate.
In the case of signs suggestive of an adrenal insufficiency, in addition to the general measures to eliminate the drug and reduce its absorption, a 100 mg dose of hydrocortisone should be administered at once, together with saline and glucose infusions.
Close surveillance will be necessary: blood pressure and fluid and electrolyte balance should be monitored for a few days.
