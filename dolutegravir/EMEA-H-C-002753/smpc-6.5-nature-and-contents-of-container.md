# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

HDPE (high density polyethylene) bottles closed with child resistant polypropylene screw closures, with a polyethylene faced induction heat seal liner.
The bottles contain 30 or 90 film-coated tablets.

Tivicay 10 mg film-coated tablets
Each bottle contains a desiccant.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[Tivicay 5 mg dispersible tablets]

The bottles contain 60 dispersible tablets and a desiccant.

A dosing cup and oral syringe, both made from polypropylene with graduation marks, are supplied with the pack.
The syringe’s plunger is made from HDPE.
