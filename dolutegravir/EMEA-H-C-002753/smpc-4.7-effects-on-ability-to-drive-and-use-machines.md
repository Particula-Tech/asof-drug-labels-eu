# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

There have been no studies to investigate the effect of dolutegravir on driving performance or the ability to operate machines.
However, patients should be informed that dizziness has been reported during treatment with dolutegravir.
The clinical status of the patient and the adverse reaction profile of dolutegravir should be borne in mind when considering the patient's ability to drive or operate machinery.
