# 2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION

Each vial contains 300 mg of ocrelizumab in 10 mL at a concentration of 30 mg/mL. The final drug
concentration after dilution is approximately 1.2 mg/mL.

Ocrelizumab is a humanised monoclonal antibody produced in Chinese Hamster Ovary cells by recombinant DNA technology.

For the full list of excipients, see section 6.1.
