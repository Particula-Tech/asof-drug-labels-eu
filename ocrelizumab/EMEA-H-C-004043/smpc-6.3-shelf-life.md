# 6.3 Shelf life

Unopened vial

18 months

Diluted solution for intravenous infusion

Chemical and physical in-use stability has been demonstrated for 24 hours at 2-8°C and subsequently for 8 hours at room temperature.

From a microbiological point of view, the prepared infusion should be used immediately.
If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and would normally not be longer than 24 hours at 2-8°C and subsequently for 8 hours at room temperature, unless dilution is undertaken in controlled and validated aseptic conditions.

In the event an intravenous infusion cannot be completed the same day, the remaining solution should be discarded.
