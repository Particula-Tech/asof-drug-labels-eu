# 6.2 Incompatibilities

Substances added to NovoRapid may cause degradation of insulin aspart, e.g. if the medicinal product contains thiols or sulphites.
This medicinal product must not be mixed with other medicinal products, except with NPH (Neutral Protamine Hagedorn) insulin and infusion fluids as described in section 4.2.
