# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

NovoRapid (insulin aspart) can be used in pregnancy.
Data from two randomised controlled clinical trials (157 and 14 insulin aspart-exposed pregnancies respectively) do not indicate any adverse effect of insulin aspart on pregnancy or on the health of the foetus/newborn when compared to insulin human (see section 5.1).

Intensified blood glucose control and monitoring of pregnant women with diabetes (type 1 diabetes, type 2 diabetes or gestational diabetes) are recommended throughout pregnancy and when contemplating pregnancy.
Insulin requirements usually fall in the first trimester and increase subsequently during the second and third trimesters.
After delivery, insulin requirements return rapidly to pre-pregnancy levels.

There are no restrictions on treatment with NovoRapid during lactation.
Insulin treatment of the nursing mother presents no risk to the baby.
However, the NovoRapid dosage may need to be adjusted.
