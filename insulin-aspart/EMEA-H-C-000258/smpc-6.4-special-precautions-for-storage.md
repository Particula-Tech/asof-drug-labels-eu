# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store at 2°C – 8°C (in a refrigerator).
Do not freeze.
In order to protect from light keep container in the outer carton.

NovoRapid Penfill cartridges in use or carried as a spare: Do not store in the refrigerator.
Do not store above 30°C.

[NovoRapid NovoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection in pre-filled pen.]

In order to protect from light keep the cap on when NovoRapid NovoLet is not in use.
NovoRapid NovoLet in use or carried as a spare : Do not store in the refrigerator.

[NovoRapid FlexPen 100 U/ml, solution for injection in pre-filled pen.]

In order to protect from light keep the cap on when NovoRapid is not in use.
NovoRapid FlexPen in use or carried as a spare: Do not store in the refrigerator.

[NovoRapid InnoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection in pre-filled pen.]

NovoRapid InnoLet in use or carried as a spare: Do not store in the refrigerator.
