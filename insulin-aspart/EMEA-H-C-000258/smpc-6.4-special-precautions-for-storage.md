# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store at 2°C – 8°C (in a refrigerator).
Do not freeze.
In order to protect from light keep the container in the outer carton.

Vials in use or carried as a spare: Do not refrigerate.
Do not store above 30°C.

[NovoRapid Penfill 100 U/ml, solution for injection in cartridge.]

NovoRapid Penfill in use or carried as a spare.
Do not refrigerate.

[NovoRapid NovoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection in pre-filled pen.]

In order to protect from light keep the cap on when NovoRapid NovoLet is not in use.

NovoRapid NovoLet in use or carried as a spare: Do not refrigerate Do not store above 30°C.

[NovoRapid FlexPen 100 U/ml, solution for injection in pre-filled pen.]

In order to protect from light keep the cap on when NovoRapid is not in use.

NovoRapid FlexPen in use or carried as a spare: Do not refrigerate.

[NovoRapid InnoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection in pre-filled pen.]

NovoRapid InnoLet in use or carried as a spare: Do not refrigerate.
