# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store at 2°C – 8°C (in a refrigerator).
Do not freeze.
In order to protect from light keep container in the outer carton.
Vials in use or carried as a spare should be kept at ambient temperature (not above 30°C) for up to 4 weeks.

[NovoRapid Penfill 100 U/ml, solution for injection in cartridge.]

Cartridges in use or carried as a spare should be kept at ambient temperature (not above 30°C) for up to 4 weeks.

[NovoRapid NovoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection in pre-filled pen.]

In order to protect from light keep the cap on when NovoRapid NovoLet is not in use.NovoLet in use or carried as a spare should be kept at ambient temperature (not above 30°C) for up to 4 weeks.

[NovoRapid FlexPen 100 U/ml, solution for injection in pre-filled pen.]

In order to protect from light keep NovoRapid FlexPen in use or carried as a spare should be kept at ambient temperature (not above 30°C) for up to 4 weeks.
