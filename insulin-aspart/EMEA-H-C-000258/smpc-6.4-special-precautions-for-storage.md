# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store in a refrigerator (2°C – 8°C).
Do not freeze.
Keep the vial in the outer carton in order to protect from light.
Vials in use or carried as a spare: Do not refrigerate.
Do not store above 30°C.

[NovoRapid Penfill 100 U/ml, solution for injection in a cartridge.]

Keep the cartridge in the outer carton in order to protect from light.
NovoRapid Penfill in use or carried as a spare: Do not refrigerate.

[NovoRapid NovoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection in a pre-filled pen.]

Keep the cap on when NovoRapid Novolet is not in use in order to protect from light.

NovoRapid NovoLet in use or carried as a spare: Do not refrigerate.

[NovoRapid FlexPen 100 U/ml, solution for injection in a pre-filled pen.]

Keep the cap on when NovoRapid FlexPen is not in use in order to protect from light.

NovoRapid FlexPen in use or carried as a spare: Do not refrigerate.

[NovoRapid InnoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection in a pre-filled pen.]

Keep the cap on when NovoRapid InnoLet is not in use in order to protect from light.

NovoRapid InnoLet in use or carried as a spare: Do not refrigerate.
