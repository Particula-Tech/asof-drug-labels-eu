# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

10 ml of solution for injection in vial (Type 1glass) closed with a disc (rubber).
Cartons of 1 or 5 vials.
Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[NovoRapid Penfill 100 U/ml, solution for injection in cartridge.]

1.5 ml and 3 ml solution for injection in cartridge (Type 1 glass), containing a piston (rubber) and closed with a disc (rubber).
Carton of 5 cartridges (1.5 ml).
Cartons of 5 or 10 cartridges (3 ml).

[NovoRapid NovoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection in prefilled syringe.]

1.5 ml of solution for injection in a multidose disposable prefilled syringe composed of a pen injector (plastic) and a cartridge (Type 1 glass) which contains a piston (rubber) and is closed with a disc (rubber).
Carton of 5 prefilled syringes.

[NovoRapid NovoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection in prefilled syringe. (SmPC 4)]

3 ml of solution for injection in a multidose disposable prefilled syringe composed of a pen injector (plastic) and a cartridge (Type 1 glass) which contains a piston (rubber) and is closed with a disc (rubber).

[NovoRapid FlexPen 100 U/ml, solution for injection in prefilled pen.]

3 ml of solution for injection in a multidose disposable prefilled pen composed of a pen injector (plastic) and a cartridge (Type 1 glass) which contains a piston (rubber) and is closed with a disc (rubber).
Cartons of 5 or 10 prefilled pens.
