# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

1 or 5 vials of 10 ml made of Type 1 glass and closed with a rubber disc.
The vials are packed in a carton.

[NovoRapid Penfill 100 U/ml, solution for injection in cartridges.]

5 NovoRapid Penfill cartridges (1.5 ml and 3ml) and 5 or 10 NovoRapid Penfill cartridges (3 ml) made of Type 1 glass, containing a rubber piston and closed with a rubber disc.
The cartridges are packed in a carton

[NovoRapid NovoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection, prefilled syringe.]

5 NovoRapid NovoLet prefilled syringes comprising a pen injector with a cartridge (1.5 ml).
The cartridge is made of Type 1 glass, contains a rubber piston and is closed with a rubber disc.
The prefilled syringes are packed in a carton.
The pen injector is made of plastic.
The prefilled multidose syringe is disposable.

[NovoRapid NovoLet 100 U/ml, solution for injection, prefilled syringe. (SmPC 4)]

5 or 10 NovoRapid NovoLet prefilled syringes comprising a pen injector with a cartridge (3 ml).
