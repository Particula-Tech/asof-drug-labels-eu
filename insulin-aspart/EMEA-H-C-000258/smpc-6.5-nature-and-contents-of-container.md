# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Cartons of 1 or 5 vials.

Cartons of 5 or 10 cartridges

Cartons of 5 or 10 pre-filled pens.

[NovoRapid FlexPen 100 U/ml, solution for injection in pre-filled pen.]

Cartons of 1, 5 or 10 pre-filled syringe pens.
