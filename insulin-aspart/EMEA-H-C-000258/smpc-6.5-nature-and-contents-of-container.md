# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

5 or 10 NovoRapid FlexPen prefilled pens comprising a pen injector with a cartridge (3ml).
The cartridge is made of Type 1 glass, contains a rubber piston and is closed with a rubber disc.
The prefilled pens are packed in a carton.
The pen injector is made of plastic.
The prefilled multidose pen is disposable.
