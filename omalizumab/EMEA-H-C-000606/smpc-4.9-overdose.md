# 4.9 Overdose

No case of overdose has been reported.
Maximum tolerated dose of Xolair has not been determined.
Single intravenous doses up to 4,000 mg have been administered to patients without evidence of dose-limiting toxicities.
The highest cumulative dose administered to patients was 44,000 mg over a 20-week period and this dose did not result in any untoward acute effects.
