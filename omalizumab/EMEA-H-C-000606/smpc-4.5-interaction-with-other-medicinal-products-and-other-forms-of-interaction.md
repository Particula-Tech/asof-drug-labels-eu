# 4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

Cytochrome P450 enzymes, efflux pumps and protein-binding mechanisms are not involved in the clearance of omalizumab; thus, there is little potential for drug-drug interactions.
No formal medicinal product or vaccine interaction studies have been performed with Xolair.
There is no pharmacological reason to expect that commonly prescribed medications used in the treatment of asthma will interact with omalizumab.

In clinical studies Xolair was commonly used in conjunction with inhaled and oral corticosteroids, inhaled short-acting and long-acting beta agonists, leukotriene modifiers, theophyllines and oral antihistamines.
There was no indication that the safety of Xolair was altered with these other commonly used asthma medications.
Limited data are available on the use of Xolair in combination with specific immunotherapy (hypo-sensitisation therapy).
Efficacy of Xolair treatment in combination with specific immunotherapy has not been established.
Current data suggest that no dose adjustments of Xolair are needed in patients on established hypo-sensitisation therapy.
