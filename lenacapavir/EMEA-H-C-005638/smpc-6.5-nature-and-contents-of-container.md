# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Sunlenca injection is packaged in a dosing kit containing:

- 2 clear glass vials, each containing 1.5 mL solution for injection.
  Vials are sealed with an elastomeric butyl rubber closure and aluminum overseal with flip off cap;
- 2 vial access devices, 2 disposable syringes, and 2 injection safety needles for subcutaneous injection (22-gauge, 12.7 mm).

[Sunlenca 300 mg film-coated tablets]

Sunlenca tablets are packaged in child-resistant clear PVC/aluminium/paperboard blister.
The blister is packaged with silica gel desiccant in a flexible laminated pouch.
Pack size of 5 tablets.
