# 4.9 Overdose

If overdose occurs the patient must be monitored for signs or symptoms of adverse reactions (see section 4.8).
Treatment of overdose with Sunlenca consists of general supportive measures including monitoring of vital signs as well as observation of the clinical status of the patient.
As lenacapavir is highly protein bound, it is unlikely to be significantly removed by dialysis.
