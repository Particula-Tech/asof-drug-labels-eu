# 4.6 Fertility, pregnancy and lactation

Women of childbearing potential
Women of childbearing potential must use an effective method of contraception while receiving everolimus.

Pregnancy
There are no or limited amount of data from the use of everolimus in pregnant women.
Studies in animals have shown reproductive toxicity effects (see section 5.3).

Everolimus is not recommended during pregnancy and in women of childbearing potential not using contraception.

Breast-feeding
It is not known whether everolimus is excreted in breast milk.
However, in rats, everolimus and/or its metabolites readily pass into the milk.
Therefore, women taking everolimus should not breast-feed.

Fertility
Based on non-clinical findings, male fertility may be compromised by treatment with everolimus (see section 5.3).
