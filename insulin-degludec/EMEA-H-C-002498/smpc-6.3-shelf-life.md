# 6.3 Shelf life

30 months.

Tresiba 100 units/mL FlexTouch/FlexPen solution for injection in pre-filled pen
After first opening or carried as a spare, the medicinal product may be stored for a maximum of 8 weeks.
Do not store above 30°C.
Can be stored in a refrigerator (2°C – 8°C).
Keep the cap on the pen in order to protect from light.

Tresiba 200 units/mL FlexTouch solution for injection in pre-filled pen
After first opening or carried as a spare, the medicinal product may be stored for a maximum of 8 weeks.
Do not store above 30°C.
Can be stored in a refrigerator (2°C – 8°C).
Keep the cap on the pen in order to protect from light.

Tresiba 100 units/mL Penfill solution for injection in cartridge
After first opening or carried as a spare, the medicinal product may be stored for a maximum of 8 weeks.
Do not store above 30°C.
Do not refrigerate.
Keep cartridges in the outer carton in order to protect from light.
