# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Do not store above 30 °C.

Keep the single-dose container in the shrink wrapped tube until ready to use in order to protect from moisture.

If the tube has been opened, the single-dose container may have been exposed to moisture.
This could cause the medicinal product to not work as expected.
Examine the shrink wrapped tube periodically.
If the tube has been opened, replace the medicinal product.
