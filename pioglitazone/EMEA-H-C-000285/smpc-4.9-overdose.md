# 4.9 Overdose

One case of overdose with pioglitazone has been reported.
A patient took 120 mg/day for four days, then 180 mg/day for seven days.
The patient did not report any clinical symptoms.

Hypoglycaemia may occur in combination with sulphonylureas or insulin.
Symptomatic and general supportive measures should be taken in case of overdose.
