# 4.8 Undesirable effects

In clinical COPD studies, approximately 16% of patients experienced adverse reactions with roflumilast (compared to 5% in placebo).
The most commonly reported adverse reactions were diarrhoea (5.9%), weight decreased (3.4%), nausea (2.9%), abdominal pain (1.9%) and headache (1.7%).
The majority of these adverse reactions were mild or moderate.
These adverse reactions mainly occurred within the first weeks of therapy and mostly resolved on continued treatment.
