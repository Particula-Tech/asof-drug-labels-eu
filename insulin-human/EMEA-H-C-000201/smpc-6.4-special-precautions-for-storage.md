# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store in a refrigerator (2°C - 8°C).
Do not freeze.
Ensure that the vial in the outer carton is not directly touching the freezer compartment or freezer packs.
Keep the vial in the outer carton in order to protect from light.
For storage conditions of in use vials, see section 6.3.
