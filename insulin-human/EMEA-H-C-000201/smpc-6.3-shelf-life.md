# 6.3 Shelf-life

2 years
Once in use, the vial may be used for up to four weeks.
It is recommended that the date of the first withdrawal from the vial be noted on the label.

[Insuman Rapid 100 IU/ml solution for injection in a cartridge]

Once in use, the cartridges may be kept for up to four weeks.
This applies irrespective of whether the cartridges are immediately put into the pen or are first carried as a spare for a while.
