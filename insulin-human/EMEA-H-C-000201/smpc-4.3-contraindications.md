Insuman Implantable
Other routes of administration (e.g. injection).

Medtronic MiniMed Implantable Pump
Hypersensitivity to titanium alloy, polysulfone or silicone materials used in the implanted components of the pump.
Use of other insulin medicinal products with the Medtronic MiniMed Implantable Pump.
Use in paediatric patients who have not reached adult size due to the large size of the pump (see section 4.2 and 4.4).
Implantation of the pump in patients who reside permanently at elevations above 2439 meters
(8000 feet) (see section 4.4).
