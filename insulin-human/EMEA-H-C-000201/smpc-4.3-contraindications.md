# 4.3 Contra-indications

Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients (see section 6.1).
There may, however, be cases where no alternative (i.e. better tolerated) insulin preparation is available and where it is not possible to dispense with insulin administration entirely.
In such cases, administration of Insuman Rapid must only be continued under close supervision and, if necessary, with concomitant anti-allergic treatment.
When transferring a patient with an allergy to animal insulin to Insuman Rapid, the possibility of immunological cross-reactions between human and animal insulin must be taken into account.
Therefore, in cases of allergy to animal insulin, intradermal skin testing is recommended before transfer to Insuman Rapid.

Insulin must not be administered in casesof manifest or imminent hypoglycaemia.

Tight blood glucose control with increased risk of hypoglycaemia may be contra-indicated in patients with significant stenoses of the coronary arteries or of the blood vessels supplying the brain (risk of cardiac or cerebral complications of hypoglycaemia) as well as in patients with proliferative retinopathy, particularly if not treated with laser (risk of amaurosis following hypoglycaemia).

Insuman Rapid must not be used in external or implanted insulin pumps or in peristaltic pumps with silicone tubing.
