# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

5 ml vial (colourless, glass type I) with flanged cap (aluminium) with inserted gasket (chlorobutyl rubber, type I, grey) and tear-off lid (polypropylene).
Content: 5 ml solution corresponding to 500 IU insulin.

Packs of 1 and 5 vials are available.

[Insuman Rapid 100 IU/ml solution for injection in a cartridge]

3.0 ml cartridge (colourless, glass type I) with plunger (bromobutyl rubber, type I, black), flanged cap (aluminium) with inserted gasket (bromobutyl rubber, type I, black).
Content: 3.0 ml solution corresponding to 300 IU insulin.

A standard pack contains 4, 5 or 10 cartridges.

[Insuman Rapid 40 IU/ml solution for injection in a vial]

10 ml vial (colourless, glass type I) with flanged cap (aluminium) with inserted gasket (chlorobutyl rubber, type I, grey) and tear-off lid (polypropylene).
Content: 10 ml solution corresponding to 400 IU insulin.
