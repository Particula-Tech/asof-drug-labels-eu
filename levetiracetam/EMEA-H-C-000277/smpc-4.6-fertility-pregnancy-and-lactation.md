# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

There are no adequate data from the use of Keppra in pregnant woman.
Studies in animals have shown reproductive toxicity (see section 5.3).
The potential risk for human is unknown.
Keppra should not be used during pregnancy unless clearly necessary.
Discontinuation of antiepileptic treatments may result in disease worsening, harmful to the mother and the foetus.

Animal data show that levetiracetam is excreted in breast milk.
No data are available on the presence of levetiracetam in human breast milk.
Therefore, breast-feeding is not recommended.
