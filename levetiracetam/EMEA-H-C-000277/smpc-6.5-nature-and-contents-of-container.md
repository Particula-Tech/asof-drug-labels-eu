# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Keppra 250 mg film-coated tablets are packaged in aluminium/PVC blisters placed into cardboard boxes containing 20, 30, 50, 60 and 100 film-coated tablets.
Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[Keppra 500 mg film-coated tablets.]

Keppra 500 mg film-coated tablets are packaged in aluminium/PVC blisters placed into cardboard boxes containing 10, 20, 30, 50, 60, 100, 120 and 200 film-coated tablets.

[Keppra 750 mg film-coated tablets.]

Keppra 750 mg film-coated tablets are packaged in aluminium/PVC blisters placed into cardboard boxes containing 20, 30, 50, 60, 80 and 100 film-coated tablets.

[Keppra 1000 mg film-coated tablets.]

Keppra 1000 mg film-coated tablets are packaged in aluminium/PVC blisters placed into cardboard boxes containing 10, 20, 30, 50, 60, 100 and 200 film-coated tablets.
