# 4.8 Undesirable effects

Pooled safety data from clinical studies showed that 46.4 % and 42.2 % of the patients experienced undesirable effects in the Keppra and placebo groups, respectively, and that 2.4 % and 2.0 % of the patients experienced serious undesirable effects in the Keppra and placebo groups, respectively.
The most commonly reported undesirable effects were somnolence, asthenia and dizziness.
In the pooled safety analysis, there was no clear dose-response relationship but incidence and severity of the central nervous system related undesirable effects decreased over time.
