# 4.8 Undesirable effects

Keppra has been administered to more than 3,000 subjects and patients.
A total of 1,023 patients with epilepsy participated in controlled clinical studies.
Pooled safety data from these studies showed that 46.4 % and 42.2 % of the patients experienced undesirable effects in the Keppra and placebo groups, respectively, and that 2.4 % and 2.0 % of the patients experienced serious undesirable effects in the Keppra and placebo groups, respectively.
The most commonly reported undesirable effects are somnolence, asthenia and dizziness.
In the pooled safety analysis, there was no clear dose-response relationship but incidence and severity of the central nervous system related undesirable effects decreased over time.

Incidence of undesirable effects considered at least possibly related to Keppra by the investigator, from controlled clinical studies involving 1,023 epileptic patients (672 patients were treated with Keppra and 351 patients with placebo) are:

Very common undesirable effects (> 10 %).

- Body as a whole: asthenia
- Nervous system: somnolence.

Common undesirable effects (> 1 %, ≤ 10 %).

- Body as a whole: accidental injury, headache
- Digestive system: anorexia, diarrhoea, dyspepsia, nausea
- Nervous system: amnesia, ataxia, convulsion, depression, dizziness, emotional lability, hostility, insomnia, nervousness, tremor, vertigo
- Skin and appendages: rash
- Special senses: diplopia.
