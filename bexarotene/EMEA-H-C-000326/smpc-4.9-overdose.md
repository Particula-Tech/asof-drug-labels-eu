# 4.9 Overdose

No clinical experience with an overdose of Targretin capsules has been reported.
Any overdose should be treated with supportive care for the signs and symptoms exhibited by the patient.

Doses up to 1000 mg/m2/day of bexarotene have been administered in clinical studies with no acute toxic effects.
Single doses of 1500 mg/kg (9000 mg/m2) and 720 mg/kg (14,400 mg/m2) were tolerated without significant toxicity in rats and dogs, respectively.
