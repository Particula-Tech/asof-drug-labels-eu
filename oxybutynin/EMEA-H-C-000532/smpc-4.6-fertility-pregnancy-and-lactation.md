# 4.6 Fertility, pregnancy and lactation

Pregnancy
There are no adequate data on the use of oxybutynin transdermal patch in pregnant women.
Studies in animals have shown minor reproductive toxicity (see section 5.3).
Kentera should not be used during pregnancy unless clearly necessary.

Breast-feeding
When oxybutynin is used during breast-feeding, a small amount is excreted in the mother’s milk.
Use of oxybutynin while breast-feeding is therefore not recommended.
