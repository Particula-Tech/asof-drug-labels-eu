# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

White low-density polyethylene (LDPE) 5 ml bottles with white LDPE tips and red high-density polyethylene (HDPE) screw caps with a protective tamper-evident ring.

Each multi-dose bottle contains 2.5 ml Ryjunea 0.1 mg/ml.
Pack sizes: 1 or 3 multi-dose bottles.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.
