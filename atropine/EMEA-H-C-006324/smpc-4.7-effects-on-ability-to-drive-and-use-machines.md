# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

Ryjunea has a moderate influence on the ability to ride bikes, drive or use machines.
Instillation of Ryjunea, may induce temporary blurred vision or other visual disturbances (see section 4.8).
Patients should be advised not to ride bikes, drive or use machines until their vision has cleared.
This effect may last up to 14 days after stopping treatment (see section 4.4).
