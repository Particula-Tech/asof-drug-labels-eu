# 6.3 Shelf life

2 years

Chemical and physical in-use stability has been demonstrated for 28 days at 2°C - 8°C.

From a microbiological point of view, once opened, the product may be stored for a maximum of 28 days at 2°C - 8°C.
The NutropinAq is designed to withstand a nominal (one hour maximum) period of time outside of the refridgerator on a daily basis.
Do not remove the cartridge that is being used from the NutropinAq Pen between injections.

After first opening: the product may be stored for up to 28 days at 2°C - 8°C
