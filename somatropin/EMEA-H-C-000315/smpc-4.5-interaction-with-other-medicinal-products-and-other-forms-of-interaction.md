# 4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

Limited published data indicate that growth hormone treatment increases cytochrome P450 mediated antipyrine clearance in man.
Monitoring is advisable when NutropinAq is administered in combination with drugs known to be metabolised by CYP450 liver enzymes, such as corticosteroids, sex steroids, anticonvulsants, and cyclosporin.
