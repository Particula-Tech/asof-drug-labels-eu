# 4.9 Overdose

Symptoms

Acute overdose could lead to hyperglycaemia.
Long-term overdose could result in signs and symptoms of gigantism and/or acromegaly consistent with the known effects of excess growth hormone.

Management

Treatment is symptomatic and supportive.
There is no antidote for somatropin overdose.
It is recommended to monitor thyroid function following an overdose.
