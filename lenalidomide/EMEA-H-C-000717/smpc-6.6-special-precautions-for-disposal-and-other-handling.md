# 6.6 Special precautions for disposal and other handling

Capsules should not be opened or crushed.
If powder from lenalidomide makes contact with the skin, the skin should be washed immediately and thoroughly with soap and water.
If lenalidomide makes contact with the mucous membranes, they should be thoroughly flushed with water.

Any unused product or waste material should be returned to the pharmacist for safe disposal in accordance with local requirements.
