# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

Pregnancy: Caelyx is embryotoxic in rats and embryotoxic and abortifacient in rabbits.
Teratogenicity cannot be ruled out.
There is no experience in pregnant women with Caelyx.
Therefore, administration to pregnant women is not recommended.
Women of child-bearing potential must be advised to avoid pregnancy while they or their male partner are receiving Caelyx and in the six months following discontinuation of Caelyx therapy (see 5.3).

Lactation: It is not known whether Caelyx is excreted in human milk and because of the potential for serious adverse reactions in nursing infants, therefore mothers must discontinue nursing prior to beginning Caelyx treatment.
Health experts recommend that HIV infected women do not breast-feed their infants under any circumstances in order to avoid transmission of HIV.
