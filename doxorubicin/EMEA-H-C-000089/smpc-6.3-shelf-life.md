# 6.3 Shelf-life

Unopened vials of material have a shelf-life of 18 months and should be stored at 2°C to 8°C.
After dilution with 5% Glucose Intravenous Infusion, the diluted Caelyx solution should be used immediately.
Diluted product not for immediate use should be prepared under aseptic conditions and in line with good pharmaceutical practice should be stored at 2°C to 8°C for no longer than 24 hours.
Partially used vials should be discarded.
