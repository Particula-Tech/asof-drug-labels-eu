# 5.1 Pharmacodynamic properties

Pharmacotherapeutic group: Antiepileptics, fatty acid derivatives, ATC code: N03AG04

Mechanism of action

Vigabatrin is a selective irreversible inhibitor of GABA transaminase, the enzyme responsible for the breakdown of GABA (gamma aminobutyric acid).
Vigabatrin increases the concentration of GABA, the major inhibitory neurotransmitter in the brain.

Clinical efficacy and safety

Controlled and long-term clinical trials have shown that vigabatrin is an effective anticonvulsant agent when given as first line treatment in patients with infantile spasms and as add-on therapy in patients with epilepsy not controlled satisfactorily by conventional therapy.
This efficacy is particularly marked in patients with seizures of partial origin.
