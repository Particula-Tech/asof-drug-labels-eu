# 4.8 Undesirable effects

In clinical studies at a dose of 250 micrograms (total 633 patients) the most frequently observed adverse reactions, at least possibly related to Aloxi, were headache (9 %) and constipation (5 %).

In the clinical studies the following adverse reactions (ARs) were observed as possibly or probably related to Aloxi.
These were classified as common (≥1/100 to <1/10) or uncommon (≥1/1,000 to <1/100).
Very rare (<1/10,000) adverse reactions were reported post-marketing.
