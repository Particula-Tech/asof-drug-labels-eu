# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

� Pregnancy

Reproduction studies performed in rats and in rabbits revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or harm to the foetus due to clopidogrel.
There are, however, no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women.
In view of the lack of data, clopidogrel is not recommended during pregnancy.

� Lactation

Studies in rats have shown that clopidogrel and/or its metabolites are excreted in the milk.
It is not known whether this medicinal product is excreted in human milk (see 4.3 Contra-indications).
