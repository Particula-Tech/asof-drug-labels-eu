# 4.9 Overdose

Signs and symptoms

There has been no experience of overdose of isatuximab in clinical studies.
Doses of intravenous isatuximab up to 20 mg/kg have been administered in clinical studies.

Management

There is no known specific antidote for SARCLISA overdose.
In the event of overdose, monitor the patients for signs or symptoms of adverse reactions and take all appropriate measures immediately.
