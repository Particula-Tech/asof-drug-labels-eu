# 6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store in a refrigerator (2°C - 8°C).
Do not freeze.
Store in the original package in order to protect from light.

For storage conditions after dilution of the medicinal product, see section 6.3.

[Sarclisa 1 400 mg solution for injection]

Unpunctured vials may be stored at room temperature (≤30°C) for a single period of up to 24 hours.
Once the vial has been taken out of the refrigerator, it must not be returned to the refrigerator (see section 6.6).

For storage conditions after insertion of the vial in CirCLIQ OBDS or of the solution in the syringe for manual administration, see section 6.3.
For storage conditions of the OBDS, please refer to CirCLIQ OBDS Instructions For Use.
