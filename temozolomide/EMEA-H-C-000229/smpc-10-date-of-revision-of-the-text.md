Temodal 5 mg hard capsules temozolomide

- The active substance is temozolomide 5 mg.
- The other ingredients of the capsule are anhydrous lactose, colloidal anhydrous silica, sodium starch glycolate, tartaric acid, stearic acid.
  Capsule shells contain gelatine, titanium dioxide, sodium lauryl sulphate and are imprinted with pharmaceutical ink: green imprint contains pharmaceutical grade shellac, propylene glycol, ammonium hydroxide, titanium dioxide (E 171), yellow iron oxide (E 172) and indigotin (E 132).

Marketing Authorization Holder: SP Europe, Rue de Stalle 73, B-1180 Bruxelles, Belgium

Manufacturer: SP Labo N.V., Industriepark 30, B-2220 Heist-op-den-Berg, Belgium

Temodal 5 mg capsules for oral use are dispensed in amber glass bottles containing 5 or 20 capsules.
Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

Temodal is an antitumour agent.

Temodal capsules are used to treat patients with specific forms of brain tumour (glioblastoma multiforme or anaplastic astrocytoma, showing recurrence or progression after standard therapy).

Do not take Temodal:

- if you are hypersensitive (allergic) to temozolomide or any of the other ingredients of Temodal.
- if you have had an allergic reaction to dacarbazine (DTIC).
- if certain kinds of blood cells are severely reduced.

Take special care with Temodal:

- if you have anaemia (low blood count) or blood clotting problems before treatment, or develop them during treatment.
  Your doctor may need to reduce the dose of your medicine or interrupt your treatment, or you may need other treatment.
  Your doctor will decide whether any change in your treatment is needed.
  In some cases, it may be necessary to stop treatment with Temodal.
  Your blood will be tested frequently to monitor your condition.
- if you vomit frequently before or during treatment.
  Ask your doctor about controlling the vomiting, and the best time to take Temodal until the vomiting is under control.
  If you vomit after taking your dose, do not take a second dose on the same day.
- do not open the capsules.
  If a capsule is damaged, avoid contact of the powder with your skin, eyes or nose.
  Avoid inhaling the powder.
  If you accidentally get some in your eyes or nose, flush the area with water.
- Temodal may be used in children three years of age and older.
  There is no experience with use of Temodal in children younger than three years old.
- Effective contraceptive precautions are recommended for male patients taking Temodal.
  Male patients are advised not to father a child for up to 6 months after stopping treatment and to seek advice on conservation of sperm prior to treatment because of the possibility of irreversible infertility due to therapy with Temodal.

Pregnancy
Tell your doctor if you are pregnant.
Temodal must not be used by a woman who is pregnant.
Effective contraceptive precautions are recommended for both male and female patients who are taking Temodal.

Breast-feeding
Do not use Temodal if you are breast-feeding.

Driving and using machines:
When you take Temodal you may feel tired or sleepy.
In this case, do not drive or operate any tools or machines.

Important information about some of the ingredients of Temodal:
Each capsule contains a small amount of lactose.

Taking other medicines:
Please inform your doctor or pharmacist if you are taking or have recently taken any other medicines, even those not prescribed.

Your doctor will determine the correct dose of Temodal for you to take, based on your size (height and weight) and whether you have had chemotherapy treatment.
You may be given other medication to take before and/or after Temodal to avoid or control nausea.

Take your prescribed dose of Temodal once a day for five days.
Take the dose on an empty stomach; for example, at least one hour before you plan to eat breakfast.
Do not open or chew the capsules.
Swallow the capsule(s) whole with a glass of water.

A treatment cycle with Temodal comprises 28 days.
You will take the capsules once daily for the first five days followed by a 23 day treatment interruption.
You will then take this medicine again once daily for five days.
Before each new treatment cycle, your blood will be tested to determine if the Temodal dose needs to be adjusted.

If you take more Temodal than you should:
Take Temodal only as it is prescribed for you.
If you accidentally take more Temodal capsules than you were told to, contact your doctor or pharmacist.
If you forget to take Temodal:
Take the missed dose as soon as possible during the same day.
If a full day has gone by, check with your doctor.
Do not double the next dose unless your doctor tells you to do so.

Like all medicines, Temodal can have side effects.

Contact your doctor immediately if you have a severe allergic reaction (hives, wheezing or other breathing difficulty).

Temodal treatment can cause a reduction in certain kinds of blood cells.
This may cause you to have increased bruising or bleeding, anaemia, fever, and/or a reduced resistance to infections.
Your doctor will monitor your blood regularly for any changes, and will decide if any specific treatment is needed.
In some cases, your Temodal dose will be reduced or discontinued.

The following undesirable effects may occur and medical attention may be required: nausea (feeling sick), vomiting, tiredness, constipation, headache, loss of appetite or weight, diarrhoea, sleepiness, fever, rash, weakness, pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, upset stomach, hair loss, chills, itching, feeling unwell, change in taste, tingling sensation, and abnormal blood values.

Very rare cases of rash with skin swelling, including on the palms of the hands and soles of the feet, have been observed.
Tell your doctor if this occurs.

If you notice any side effects not mentioned in this leaflet, please inform your doctor or pharmacist.
