# 6.1 List of excipients

The capsule contains:
anhydrous lactose, colloidal anhydrous silica, sodium starch glycolate, tartaric acid, stearic acid.

Capsule shells contain:
gelatine, titanium dioxide (E 171), sodium lauryl sulphate, yellow iron oxide (E 172), indigo carmine (E 132),

and are imprinted with black pharmaceutical ink, which contains:
shellac, propylene glycol, purified water, ammonium hydroxide, potassium hydroxide, black iron oxide (E 172).

[Temodal 20 mg hard capsules]

gelatine, titanium dioxide (E 171), sodium lauryl sulphate, yellow iron oxide (E 172)

[Temodal 100 mg hard capsules]

gelatine, titanium dioxide (E 171), sodium lauryl sulphate, red iron oxide (E172).

[Temodal 140 mg hard capsules]

gelatine, titanium dioxide (E 171), sodium lauryl sulphate , indigo carmine (E 132),

and are imprinted with black pharmaceutical ink which contains:

[Temodal 180 mg hard capsules]

gelatine, titanium dioxide (E 171), sodium lauryl sulphate, yellow iron oxide (E 172), red iron oxide (E 172)

[Temodal 250 mg hard capsules]

gelatine, titanium dioxide (E 171), sodium lauryl sulphate
