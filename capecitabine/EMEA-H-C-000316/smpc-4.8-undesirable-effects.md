# 4.8 Undesirable effects

The most frequent undesirable effects reported in two phase III trials in colorectal cancer (596 patients) were gastrointestinal disorders, especially diarrhoea, nausea, vomiting and stomatitis, and hand-foot syndrome.
Hand-foot syndrome (palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia) is characterised by numbness, dysesthesia / paresthesia, tingling, painless or painful swelling or erythema, desquamation, blistering, or severe pain.
The following headings are used to rank the undesirable effects by frequency: Very common (>1/10), common (>1/100, <1/10), uncommon (>1/1,000, <1/100), rare (>1/10,000, <1/1,000) and very rare (<1/10,000, including isolated reports).

Gastrointestinal
Very common: Diarrhoea (48%, severe 13%), nausea (38%, severe 3%), vomiting (23%, severe 3%), abdominal pain (11%, severe 3%) and stomatitis (24%, severe 2%).
Common: Constipation (7%, severe 0.3%), dyspepsia (5%, severe 0.2%), upper abdominal pain (6%, severe 1%) dry mouth (4%), flatulence (4%) and loose stools (3%).
Rare: Events related to inflammation/ulceration of mucous membranes such as esophagitis, gastritis, duodenitis, colitis, and gastrointestinal hemorrhage.

Cutaneous
Very common: Hand-foot syndrome or palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia (53%, severe 17%), dermatitis (10%, severe 0.2%).
Common: Alopecia (6%), dry skin (7%, severe 0.2%), rash erythematous (5%, severe 0.2%), skin hyperpigmentation (4%, severe 0.2%), pruritus (3%, severe 0.3%), nail disorders (3%) and localised exfoliation (2%).
Rare: Photosensitivity reactions, radiation recall syndrome, onycholysis, brittle nails, nail discoloration, nail dystrophy, and skin fissures

General undesirable effects
Very common: Fatigue (21%, severe 1.2%).
Common: Pyrexia (8%, severe 0.2%), weakness (7%, severe 0.5%), asthenia (4%, severe 0.5%), lethargy (3%, severe 0.2%) and pain in limb (3%).

Neurologic
Common: Headache (5%), paraesthesia (3%), taste disturbance (5%), dizziness (5%), insomnia (3%), and hyperesthesia (2%).
The incidence of severe effects never exceeded 0.3% for any of these symptoms.
The majority of paresthesia events occurred in the concomitant presence of hand-foot syndrome.
Rare: Encephalopathy, confusion and cerebellar signs such as ataxia, dysarthria, impaired balance and abnormal co-ordination.

Cardiovascular
Common: Lower limb oedema (4%).
Uncommon: Chest pain, angina pectoris, myocardial infarction.
Rare: Cardiac failure, cardiac arrest, cardiomyopathy, sudden death, tachycardia, atrial arrhythmias including atrial fibrillation, and ventricular extrasystoles.

Hepatobiliary
Rare: Abnormal liver function tests, jaundice
Very rare: hepatic failure, cholestatic hepatitis

Haematologic
Common: Grade 3 or 4 laboratory abnormalities (according to NCIC/CTC), decrease in hemoglobin (2%) and neutropenia (2%).
Uncommon: Thrombocytopenia
Rare: Bone marrow depression and pancytopenia.
Events related to bone marrow depression, immune system compromise, and/or disruption of mucous membranes such as local and fatal systemic infections (including bacterial, viral, fungal etiologies) and sepsis.

Biochemical laboratory abnormalities
Very common: Grade 3 or 4 (according to NCIC/CTC) bilirubin increases (23%).
Common: Grade 3 or 4 alkaline phosphatase, (3%).
Hyperglycemia (6%).

Others
Very common: Anorexia (10%, severe 0.5%).
Common: Dehydration (4%, severe 1.7%), dyspnea (4%, severe 0.2%), increased lacrimation (6%), conjunctivitis (3%), decreased appetite (7%), epistaxis (2%), weight decrease (3%), back pain (3%) and arthralgia (2%) and depression (2%),.
The incidence of severe cases of all these undesirable effects never exceeded 0.3%.
Rare: Chest pain, myalgias, eye irritation and cough
