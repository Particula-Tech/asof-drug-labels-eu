# 4.8 Undesirable effects

Xeloda monotherapy: The most frequent treatment-related undesirable effects reported in two phase III trials and one phase II trial in colorectal cancer (630 patients) as well as four phase II trials in breast cancer (319 patients) were gastrointestinal disorders, especially diarrhoea, nausea, vomiting and stomatitis, and hand-foot syndrome (palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia).
The following headings are used to rank the undesirable effects by frequency: Very common (>1/10), common (>1/100, <1/10), uncommon (>1/1,000, <1/100), rare (>1/10,000, <1/1,000) and very rare (<1/10,000, including isolated reports).

Gastrointestinal
Very common: Diarrhoea (50%, severe 13%), nausea (43%, severe 4%), vomiting (28%, severe 4%), stomatitis (25%, severe 4%) and abdominal pain (12%, severe 3%).
Common: Constipation (8%, severe 1 %), upper abdominal pain (7%, severe 1%), dyspepsia (6% ), dry mouth (4%), flatulence (4%), loose stools (3%) and oral pain (1%).
Uncommon: Events related to inflammation/ulceration of mucous membranes such as esophagitis, gastritis, duodenitis, colitis, and gastrointestinal hemorrhage.

Cutaneous
Very common: Hand-foot syndrome or palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia (53%, severe 16%), dermatitis (10%).
Common: Dry skin (7%), alopecia (5%), rash erythematous (6%), skin hyperpigmentation (3%), pruritus (3%), nail disorders (2%), localised exfoliation (2%), pigmentation disorder (1%), skin fissures (1%), exfoliative dermatitis (1%), pruritic rash (1%), skin discoloration (1%).
Uncommon: Photosensitivity reactions, radiation recall syndrome, onycholysis, brittle nails, nail discoloration and nail dystrophy.

General undesirable effects
Very common: Fatigue (24%, severe 3%).
Common: Pyrexia (9%), weakness (6%, severe 1%), asthenia (4%, severe 1%), pain in limb (4%), lethargy (3%), rigors (2%) and malaise (1%) .

Neurologic
Common: Headache (5%), paraesthesia (5%), dizziness (5%), taste disturbance (4%), insomnia (4%), hypoesthesia (2%) and hyperesthesia (2%).
The incidence of severe effects never exceeded 0.5% for any of these symptoms.
The majority of paresthesia events occurred in the concomitant presence of hand-foot syndrome.
Uncommon: Encephalopathy, confusion and cerebellar signs such as ataxia, dysarthria, impaired balance and abnormal co-ordination.

Cardiovascular
Common: Lower limb oedema (4%).
Uncommon: Chest pain, angina pectoris, myocardial infarction, cardiac failure, cardiac arrest, cardiomyopathy, sudden death, tachycardia, atrial arrhythmias including atrial fibrillation, and ventricular extrasystoles.

Hepatobiliary
Uncommon: Abnormal liver function tests, jaundice
Very rare: hepatic failure, cholestatic hepatitis

Haematologic
Common: Grade 3 or 4 laboratory abnormalities (according to NCIC/CTC), decrease in hemoglobin (3%) neutropenia (3%) and thrombocytopenia (2%).
Uncommon: Bone marrow depression and pancytopenia.
Rare:.
Events related to bone marrow depression, immune system compromise, and/or disruption of mucous membranes such as local and fatal systemic infections (including bacterial, viral, fungal etiologies) and sepsis.

Biochemical laboratory abnormalities
Very common: Grade 3 or 4 (according to NCIC/CTC) bilirubin increases (17%).
Common: Grade 3 or 4 alkaline phosphatase (3%).
Uncommon: Hyperglycemia.

Others
Very common: Anorexia (11%, severe 1%).
Common: Dehydration (6%, severe 3%), dyspnea (4%, severe 2%), increased lacrimation (6%), conjunctivitis (3%), decreased appetite (7%), epistaxis (2%), weight decrease (3%), back pain (3%) and arthralgia (2%) depression (2%), myalgia (2%), eye irritation (2%), cough (2%), and vertigo (1%).
The incidence of severe cases of all these undesirable effects never exceeded 0.5%.
Uncommon: Chest pain.
