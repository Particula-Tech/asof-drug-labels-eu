# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

Herceptin may have a minor influence on the ability to drive or use machines (see section 4.8).
Patients experiencing infusion-related symptoms (see section 4.4) should be advised not to drive and use machines until symptoms abate.

[Herceptin 600 mg solution for injection in vial]

Patients experiencing administration-related symptoms (see section 4.4) should be advised not to drive and use machines until symptoms abate.
