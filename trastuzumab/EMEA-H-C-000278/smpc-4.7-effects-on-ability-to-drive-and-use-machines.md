# 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

.
Herceptin has no or negligible influence on the ability to drive or use machines.
However, patients experiencing infusion-related symptoms (see section 4.4) should be advised not to drive and use machines until symptoms abate.

[Herceptin 600 mg/5 mL solution for injection]

However, patients experiencing administration-related symptoms (see section 4.4) should be advised not to drive and use machines until symptoms abate.
