# 4.1 Therapeutic indications

Herceptin is indicated for the treatment of patients with metastatic breast cancer whose tumours overexpress HER2:

a) as monotherapy for the treatment of those patients who have received at least two chemotherapy regimens for their metastatic disease.
Prior chemotherapy must have included at least an anthracycline and a taxane unless patients are unsuitable for these treatments.
Hormone receptor positive patients must also have failed hormonal therapy, unless patients are unsuitable for these treatments.

b) in combination with paclitaxel for the treatment of those patients who have not received chemotherapy for their metastatic disease and for whom an anthracycline is not suitable.

c) in combination with docetaxel for the treatment of those patients who have not received chemotherapy for their metastatic disease.

Herceptin should only be used in patients whose tumours have either HER2 overexpression or HER2 gene amplification as determined by an accurate and validated assay (see 4.4 and 5.1).
