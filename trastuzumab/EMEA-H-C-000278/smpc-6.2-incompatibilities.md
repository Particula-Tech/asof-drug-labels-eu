# 6.2 Incompatibilities

This medicinal product must not be mixed or diluted with other medicinal products except those mentioned under section 6.6.
Do not dilute with glucose solutions since these cause aggregation of the protein.

[Herceptin 600 mg solution for injection in vial]

Herceptin subcutaneous formulation is a ready to use solution which should not be mixed or diluted with other products.

[Herceptin 600 mg solution for injection in administration system]

Herceptin subcutaneous formulation in administration system is ready to use.
