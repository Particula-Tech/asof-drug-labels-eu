# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Cartons containing 1, 3, or 6 blister strips (10 x 1, 30 x 1, 60 x 1) in perforated aluminium unit dose blisters.
The blister consists of an aluminium lidding foil coated with polyvinylchloride-polyvinylacetate copolymer-acrylate (PVCAC acrylate) in contact with the product and an aluminium bottom foil with polyvinylchloride (PVC) in contact with the product.

Polypropylene bottle with a screw cap containing 60 hard capsules.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.
