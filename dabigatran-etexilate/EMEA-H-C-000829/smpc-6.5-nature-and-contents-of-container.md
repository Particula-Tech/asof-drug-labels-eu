# 6.5 Nature and contents of container

Cartons containing 10 x 1, 30 x 1 or 60 x 1 hard capsules in perforated aluminium unit dose blisters.
Furthermore, cartons containing 6 blister strips (60 x 1) in perforated aluminium unit dose white blisters.

Polypropylene bottle with a screw cap containing 60 hard capsules.

Not all pack sizes may be marketed.

[Pradaxa 110 mg hard capsules]

Cartons containing 10 x 1, 30 x 1 or 60 x 1 hard capsules a multipack containing 3 packs of 60 x 1 hard capsules (180 hard capsules) and a multipack containing 2 packs of 50 x 1 hard capsules (100 hard capsules) in perforated aluminium unit dose blisters.

[Pradaxa 150 mg hard capsules]

Cartons containing 10 x 1, 30 x 1 or 60 x 1 hard capsules,a multipack containing 3 packs of 60 x 1 hard capsules (180 hard capsules) and a multipack containing 2 packs of 50 x 1 hard capsules (100 hard capsules) in perforated aluminium unit dose blisters.
