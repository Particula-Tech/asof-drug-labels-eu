# 4.9 Overdose

From clinical trials and marketed use, overdoses have been reported of up to 40 times (72 mg) the recommended maintenance dose.
Events reported included severe nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea and severe hypoglycaemia.
In the event of overdose, appropriate supportive treatment should be initiated according to the patient’s clinical signs and symptoms.
The patient should be observed for clinical signs of dehydration and blood glucose should be monitored.
