# 4.8 Undesirable effects

In clinical studies involving over 1500 patients treated with AZOPT as monotherapy or adjunctive therapy to timolol maleate 0.5%, the most frequently reported treatment-related adverse events and local symptoms were: taste perversion (bitter or unusual taste) (5.3%) and temporary blurred vision upon instillation, lasting from a few seconds to a few minutes (4.8%) (see also 4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines).

The following undesirable effects definitely, probably or possibly related to treatment were reported during the clinical trials with AZOPT.
Their incidence was either common (less than 10.0%) or uncommon (less than 1.0%).

Ocular effects

Common: blurred vision, ocular discomfort (transient burning or stinging upon instillation), foreign body sensation and ocular hyperaemia.

Uncommon: dry eye, ocular pain, ocular discharge, ocular pruritus, keratitis, blepharitis, conjunctivitis, lid margin crusting, sticky sensation, tearing, eye fatigue, keratopathy, conjunctival follicles and abnormal vision.

The following ocular effects were only seen in AZOPT adjunctive studies with timolol:

Uncommon: corneal erosion.

Systemic effects

AZOPT is a sulphonamide inhibitor of carbonic anhydrase with systemic absorption.
Other undesirable effects that are attributable to this class of medicinal products are possible although they have not been observed in clinical trials.

Taste perversion (bitter or unusual taste in the mouth following instillation) was the most frequently reported systemic undesirable effect associated with the use of AZOPT during clinical studies.
It is likely caused by passage of the eye drops in the nasopharynx via the nasolacrimal canal.
Nasolacrimal occlusion or gently closing the eyelid after instillation may help reducing the incidence of this effect (see also 4.2 Posology and method of administration).

Body as a whole

Uncommon: chest pain and alopecia.

Gastrointestinal effects

Common: taste perversion (bitter or unusual taste).

Uncommon: dry mouth, nausea, and dyspepsia.

Hypersensitivity reactions

Uncommon: dermatitis.

Nervous System effects

Common: headache.

Uncommon: paresthesia, characterised as numbness and a tingling sensation of the extremities, depression, and dizziness.

Respiratory effects

Uncommon: rhinitis, dyspnoea, pharyngitis, bronchitis, epistaxis and hemoptysis.

Gastrointestinal, nervous system, haematological, renal and metabolic effects are generally associated with systemic carbonic anhydrase inhibitors.
The same type of undesirable effects that are attributable to oral carbonic anhydrase inhibitors may occur with topical administration.
