# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

Pregnancy
There are no adequate data from the use of brinzolamide in pregnant women.
Studies in animals have shown reproductive toxicity (see 5.3).
The potential risk for humans is unknown.
AZOPT should not be used during pregnancy unless clearly necessary.

Nursing mothers
It is not known whether brinzolamide is excreted in human milk, however, this substance is excreted in rat milk.
It is strongly recommended to avoid the use of AZOPT when breast-feeding.
