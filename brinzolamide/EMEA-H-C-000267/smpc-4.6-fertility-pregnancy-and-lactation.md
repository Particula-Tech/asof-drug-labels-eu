# 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation

Pregnancy
There are no or limited amount of data from the use of brinzolamide in pregnant women.
Studies in animals have shown reproductive toxicity (see also section 5.3).
AZOPT is not recommended during pregnancy and in women of childbearing potential not using contraception.

Lactation
It is not known whether brinzolamide/metabolites are excreted in human milk.
Animal studies have shown the excretion of brinzolamide in breast milk.
Brinzolamide should only be used during breast-feeding when the benefit of breast-feeding for the child and the benefit of therapy for the woman outweigh the possible risks.
