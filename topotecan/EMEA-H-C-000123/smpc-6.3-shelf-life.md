# 6.3 Shelf-life

Vials
3 years.

Reconstituted and diluted solutions
The product should be used immediately after reconstitution as it contains no antibacterial preservative.
If reconstitution and dilution is performed under strict aseptic conditions (e.g. an LAF bench) the product should be used (infusion completed) within 12 hours at room temperature or 24 hours if stored at 2-8 0C after the first breakage.
