# 4.3 Contraindications

Topotecan is contra-indicated in patients who
− have a history of severe hypersensitivity to topotecan or to any of the excipients
− are pregnant or breast feeding (see section 4.6 Pregnancy and lactation)
− already have severe bone marrow depression prior to starting first course, as evidenced by baseline neutrophils < 1.5 x 109/l and/or a platelet count of ≤ 100 x 109/l.
