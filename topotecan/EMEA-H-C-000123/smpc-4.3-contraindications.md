# 4.3 Contraindications

Topotecan is contra-indicated in patients who

- have a history of severe hypersensitivity reactions to topotecan and/or its excipients
- are pregnant or breast feeding
- already have severe bone marrow depression prior to starting first course, as evidenced by

baseline neutrophils < 1.5 x 109/l and/or a platelet count of ≤ 100 x 109/l.
