# 4.3 Contraindications

- Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients listed in section 6.1.
- Narrow-angle glaucoma.
- Phaeochromocytoma.
- Co-administration with non-selective monoamine oxidase (MAO) inhibitors.
  These inhibitors should already be discontinued for at least two weeks prior to initiating therapy due to the established underlying levodopa therapy (see section 4.5).
- A previous history of neuroleptic malignant syndrome (NMS) and/or non-traumatic rhabdomyolysis.
