# 4.9 Overdose

The acute symptoms of levodopa overdose can be expected to arise from dopaminergic overstimulation.
Using more than one dose of Inbrija (2 capsules) to treat the same OFF period may result in CNS disturbances, with an increasing likelihood of cardiovascular disturbance (e.g. hypotension, tachycardia) and more severe psychiatric problems at higher doses.

Patients should be monitored and supportive care should be provided.
Patients should receive electrocardiographic monitoring for the development of arrhythmias; if needed, appropriate antiarrhythmic therapy should be given.
