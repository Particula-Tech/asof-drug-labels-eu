# 5.3 Preclinical safety data

Long-term studies in animals to evaluate the carcinogenic potential of bupivacaine have not been conducted.
The mutagenic potential of bupivacaine has not been determined.

Bupivacaine crosses the placenta.
Bupivacaine produced developmental toxicity when administered subcutaneously to pregnant rats and rabbits at clinically relevant doses.
An increase in embryo-foetal deaths in rabbits and decreased survival of the offspring in rats was observed.
The effect on fertility of bupivacaine has not been determined.
